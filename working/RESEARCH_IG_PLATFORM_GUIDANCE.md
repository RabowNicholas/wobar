# INSTAGRAM'S OWN GUIDANCE — T1

> **Why this file exists.** [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §5 needs a definition of *good* that isn't invented in this vault. Instagram publishes one. Everything here is Instagram's own words or a direct paraphrase — **no practitioner blogs, no agency benchmarks, no inference.** Where Instagram is silent, this file says so rather than filling the gap.

---

## §1 — THE THREE METRICS INSTAGRAM SAYS TO WATCH

Verbatim from the Insights page, under *"Metrics worth paying attention to"*:

| Metric | Instagram's stated reason |
|---|---|
| **Shares** | *"indicate how valuable or relatable your content is, and amplify your reach to new audiences"* |
| **Comments** | *"tell you if your content is resonating and sparking conversations"* |
| **Follows** | *"show you're reaching new audiences who love your content and want to see more like it. You can even track follows that come from posts and reels"* |

**What is NOT on that list: likes, views, reach, follower count, engagement rate.**

**Views is framed as an outcome, not a focus.** The growth indicator Instagram names is **Viewers** — unique people — not view count: *"Seeing growth in Viewers is a win-win. It means that your content is performing well, and that it's getting shown more frequently to more people."*

### Why this matters more than a normal source
**All three are *who* metrics.** A share is a person choosing to hand it to a specific other person. A comment is an identifiable account. A follow is an identifiable account. **None of the three is a volume counter, and all three are attributable to a post.**

That is the same shape as [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §5's *tier 2 outranks tier 1* — arrived at independently, from the platform's side. **The vault's instinct and Instagram's published advice point at the same three numbers.** Treat that convergence as the strongest available support for §5's design.

## §2 — RANKING SIGNALS

Instagram, on how reels get recommended:

> *"We use signals to predict which reels people might like. Signals include user activity like **watch time, retention, shares, likes, and comments**. We also use **audience-matching techniques** to compare people with similar interests, because what one person is engaging with can help us learn what someone else might be into."*

**And there is no single algorithm:**

> *"Instagram doesn't have a singular algorithm… There are different recommendation systems for each part of Instagram such as Feed, Stories, Explore, Reels, Search and more."*

⚠ **A reel and a grid post are ranked by different systems.** Comparing their numbers to each other is comparing two games.

## §3 — THE DISTRIBUTION LADDER, AND THE FOLLOWER-COUNT CLAIM

> *"When you post a reel… it's shown to both followers and non-followers. Among non-followers, your reel is initially shown to a small audience of people we think will enjoy it. The best-performing reels are then shown to a slightly wider audience, then the best of those are shown to an even wider group, and so on. This process is designed to level the playing field by **distributing content based on its engagement, not just the creator's follower count**."*

⚠ **Read this precisely — it bounds a claim in [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §1.**

The plan holds that a strong account is what buys peer credibility. **That survives** — credibility is a human judgement made by a producer looking at a profile, and Instagram has nothing to say about it.

**What does not survive is any version of the claim that a bigger account is needed to *reach* people.** Instagram states the opposite explicitly. Reach is earned per-post, from a cold start, every time. **Follower count is the credential, not the distribution.** Do not let the two blur together.

**Practical consequence:** each post re-enters the ladder on its own merits. There is no accumulated reach advantage to protect, which means **a format experiment costs nothing but the post.**

## §4 — WHAT INSTAGRAM SAYS HELPS REACH

- **Authentic interactions** — *"One of the most important ways to increase your reach is through engagement from your audience."*
- **Video quality** — minimum **720p**, minimum **30fps**, well-lit.
- **Trending audio** — *"We consider the popularity of trends on Reels, including music and sounds, to determine how to rank and recommend content."*
- **Reply to comments within the first couple of days** — stated as a reach lever, not just etiquette.
- **Study your insights** — Account and Content Insights, to understand who views and what they enjoy.

### ⚠ Two of these cut against a music artist, and the cost should be taken knowingly

**Trending audio.** Instagram ranks partly on trend participation. **An artist posting original music is declining a ranking signal by definition.** That is a real, permanent tax on this account — not a mistake to fix. Worth knowing so a weak result on a music post isn't misread as the music being weak.

**Optimize for sound off** — *"Use text and closed captioning whenever possible."* Instagram's advice assumes sound is optional. **For this account the sound is the product.** The resolution is not to ignore it: on-screen text carries the *hook*, so the muted scroller stops, and the sound then does the work. Text as the door, not the substitute.

## §5 — WHAT INSTAGRAM SAYS HURTS REACH

Hard rules, stated as things that *limit reach or prevent recommendation entirely*:

| Rule | Note for Wobar |
|---|---|
| **Reels over 3 minutes are not recommended to new audiences** | Hard ceiling. Full tracks and set clips must land under 3:00 or they forfeit non-follower distribution |
| **Third-party watermarks may prevent recommendation** | ⚠ **Directly relevant to the TikTok crosspost in [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §6.** Never export from TikTok and upload here — always post the clean master to both |
| **Non-recommendable content, incl. engagement bait** | Governed by Instagram's Recommendation Guidelines |
| **Reposting others' content / taking credit for what isn't yours** | *"Once detected, it will prevent your content from being recommended"* |

## §6 — WHAT INSTAGRAM SAYS MAKES A REEL ENGAGING

- **Relatable is shareable** — *"If people see your content and think 'that's literally me,' they're more likely to like it or pass it along."*
- **Start a conversation** — pose questions, ask for ideas.
- **The first 3 seconds are the gate** — *"hook your audience within the first 3 seconds… then find creative ways to keep their attention until the end."*
- **Optimize for sound off** — text and closed captioning.

**Reels are >50% of all time spent on Instagram** (Instagram's figure). Consistent with this account's own data: **95.7% of views and 92.3% of interactions are reels.**

## §7 — WHAT THIS CHANGES FOR WOBAR

1. **§5's measurement design is validated from the platform side.** Shares, comments, follows — all attributable, all *who* metrics. Build the log around those three and Instagram's advice and the vault's strategy stop being two things.
2. **Likes drop out of the picture entirely.** Instagram doesn't list them as a focus metric. Neither should the review.
3. **Replying to comments pays twice.** Instagram calls it a reach lever; [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §4 calls comment-reading the triage habit and ranks skipping it as **leak #1, the thing most likely to kill the funnel.** The same ten minutes buys distribution *and* targeting. **That is the cheapest identified action in the entire social plan.**
4. **Follower count is credential, not distribution** (§3). Keep the two separate in every future argument.
5. **Two permanent taxes, knowingly paid:** original audio forgoes the trending-audio signal, and a sound-first product fights a sound-off platform. Neither is fixable; both should be priced in before judging a music post's numbers.
6. **The 3-minute cap and the watermark rule are free wins** — pure mechanics, no strategy required, and violating either silently costs all non-follower reach.

## §8 — WHAT INSTAGRAM DOES NOT SAY

- **No published benchmarks.** Nothing on what a "good" share rate, comment rate or follow rate looks like at any account size. **There is no external answer to *"is this number good?"*** — [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §7.4's missing peer-tier comp set remains the only route to one.
- **No weighting.** The signals are listed, never ranked or quantified. Any claim that a share is worth *N* likes is not first-party.
- **Nothing on reach → follow conversion**, which is the actual question this account cares about.
- **Nothing on posting frequency, time of day, or hashtags** in the reach guidance.
- **The `/best-practices` library is video-only** — ~60 episodes incl. *"Why shares matter"*, *"Which metrics to focus on"*, *"Ranking explained"* (Mosseri), *"What limits reach"*. **Not transcribed here.** If any claim in this file needs deepening, those are the T1 sources to watch.
