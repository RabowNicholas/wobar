# HOHLWEG VISUALIZER

One unbroken camera push down a sunken lane at night, for the length of the EP. Photoreal and nearly dark; moonlight gives the base read; everything else is lit by wireframe forms inside the scene that fire from the music. The composite system (VISUAL_RESET §2.5) in motion.

## 1. Decided (Nick, 2026-10-12)

- **Tracks: ERNSTFALL · ABGRUND · GRUND. No ATEM in the visualizer.** ATEM has been cut (Nick, 2026-10-19).
- **Lit elements are wireframe forms only.** Real geometry, per the §2.5 build rule. No other fixture type.
- **The cover's form does not appear.** ⚠ Superseded 2026-10-14: it appears once, in the clearing, as what the piece arrives at — see "The cover's form at the end" in §2.
- **The lane opens into a clearing at the end, and the clearing is reached.** Nick: *"the songs naturally resolve."* The visualizer arrives. ⚠ This is newer than [[working/HOHLWEG_EP]], which still describes a record that withholds arrival (and rejected `LICHTUNG` as a title on that ground) — that doc has not been revised to match.
- **The camera is indifferent to the music except in speed.** Speed follows song tempo, including ERNSTFALL's 174 → 140. Nothing else about the camera reacts — no shake hits, no cuts, no ignite-everything at the drop. Keyframed by hand when the full-length camera is built.
- ~~**MIDI drives discrete ignitions; audio bands drive continuous breathing** of the form in view.~~ Superseded: the forms flash on kick and snare only, read from the drum **stems** (they carry the filter automation MIDI would lose); see §2 *Real hits from the stems*.
- **Vertical has to work, not only 16:9.** The lane and every form are composed centre-safe from the first blockout.

- **The place is a German forest at night** (Nick, 2026-10-13: the titles are German, so model the German landscape — "german moonlit"). Species is otherwise free; the cover's broadleaf lane is not a requirement. Working read: a fir/spruce/pine forest of the Black Forest kind — needle litter, moss, ferns, exposed roots.
- **Free assets until the build is proven; a paid tree pack is acceptable after that.**

**Carried from the course:** EEVEE · 24 fps · no modeling, assets only · moon as light source only, never in frame · fog and mist · M1 Max 32 GB.

## 2. Build stages

Each stage ends at **STOP** — Nick looks, decides, and only then does the next stage start. Stages 1–7 are all on a 100 m proof segment; nothing is built at full length until the benchmark is in.

| # | Stage | STOP — what Nick decides |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **File + scene setup.** Save the .blend, metric scale, 24 fps, EEVEE, AgX, collections, 16:9 camera with a 9:16 safe guide. | Where the file lives |
| 1 | **Spine + grey blockout.** One Bezier curve; a lane cross-section swept along it; walls 3–5 m; camera riding the same curve at constant speed. No assets, no materials. | Lane width, wall height, how much it curves, lens, how it sits in vertical |
| 2 | **Asset kit.** A small set of soil, roots, undergrowth, rocks, trees — pulled as candidates, shown side by side. | Which assets are in the kit |
| 3 | **Dress the segment.** Ground and wall materials, scatter with density masks (banks dense, path clear), canopy. | Density, and whether it reads as the cover's lane |
| 4 | **Moonlight, darkness, fog.** Base light only. Claude sets up the rig; Nick tunes it by eye. | The look of the dark — blacks deep, still readable |
| 5 | **One wireframe form.** Real beams, emissive material + real light on one control value, lighting the fog and walls around it. | The form, its scale, its colour, how far its light reaches |
| 6 | **Music on the segment.** Track in the timeline; one band stem baked to the form's breathing; one MIDI clip to an ignition. Includes the ERNSTFALL tempo-map drift test. | Whether sync and response feel right |
| 7 | **Benchmark.** Five frames at final quality, in both aspect ratios → seconds per frame → full-length render time. | Quality settings, and how vertical is delivered (crop from a larger render vs a second render) |
| 8 | **Full length.** Extend the spine; depth profile per track; the clearing; sectioning; forms placed along it; camera speed keyed to tempo. Built and reviewed one track at a time. | A stop after each track |
| 9 | **Render + finish.** Chunked image-sequence render, assembly, grade in Resolve. | Final sign-off |

**Stage 1 signed off 2026-10-13** — file `/Volumes/music/blender/hohlweg_1.blend`. Proof segment: 100 m straight-line (105.7 m along the path), three bends swinging ~5 m, tightest radius 18 m · path 2.5 m · walls 3.5 m, banks steepening · forest floor 15 m out each side (capped by the bend radius) · eye height 1.6 m · 28 mm · aim 8 m ahead on the spine · 1.37 m/s placeholder speed, frames 1–1714. Everything hangs off two curves: `Spine` and `LaneProfile`.

**Vertical is not a centre crop.** Tested on the blockout: the crop keeps ~23° of horizontal view and loses a wall in every bend; a native 9:16 render from the same camera holds both. Delivery method (two renders vs one square master) is decided at the stage 7 benchmark.

**Stage 2 kit, 2026-10-13 — all Poly Haven, CC0, free.** Path: `mud_forest`. Banks and floor: `forrest_ground_03` (needle litter) as base, `forest_leaves_02` (moss) blended in. Roots and debris: `root_cluster_01`, `pine_roots`, `tree_stump_01`, `dead_tree_trunk_02`, `dry_branches_medium_01`. Undergrowth: `fern_02`, `moss_01`, `nettle_plant`, `shrub_02`. Trees: `fir_tree_01` and `fir_sapling_medium`, lower-detail versions only. About 0.9M polygons for the whole kit. Chosen in daylight line-ups — the real test is on the lane under moonlight.

**The lane sits deep inside a dense forest** (Nick, 2026-10-13: "this lane should exist in a dense forest. obviously you cant see too much of it, but that is the look we are after"). This supersedes the earlier "open sky" note from the same day: trunks crowd both rims and the crowns nearly close overhead, leaving a broken strip of sky. From inside the trench the forest is mostly a wall of trunks at the rim and canopy above — the far forest is hidden by the walls, so it is only planted to ~30 m. **Consequence for stage 4:** direct moonlight barely reaches the path; a daylight sun was blocked almost completely in the first test.

**Known gap in the kit:** no hanging roots. Poly Haven's root assets are scanned slabs of ground; the cover's roots spilling down the bank would need a different asset.

**Stage 3 direction (Nick, 2026-10-13):** density sparse to moderate — undergrowth mostly vanishes at night and each form needs dark, plain ground around it to light · the path is littered with needles and twigs, not clean mud · Claude does the first pass on everything, hero pieces included, and Nick steers by comment only ("i want to test you doing everything with just my guidance") · a rough placeholder moon exists for checking each step in the dark; the real lighting is still stage 4.

**Bank foliage is heavy, not sparse** (Nick, 2026-10-13, on seeing the first pass: "we need a lot more foliage on the banks" and "trees would also be growing there"). This supersedes "sparse to moderate" above: the banks are overgrown with ferns, shrubs and young firs, and firs root on the bank slopes themselves, not only at the rim. The path stays clear.

**The path — what the lane floor says (Nick, 2026-10-13).** A Hohlweg is worn, not dug, so the floor carries the evidence of passage. Decided: **cart ruts** (two tracks, a ridge between) · **deserted for a long time and only now found** — overgrown, not in use · **wet: a clear night right after rainfall** — standing water in the ruts, wet mud, wet leaves · **the forest has reclaimed a lot** — moss and growth on the centre ridge, litter gathered in the ruts and edges, fallen branches across the track · erosion has **exposed roots and stones** · the **edges are ragged**, with soil slumped off the banks. Constraint: the camera rides the centre line at 1.6 m, so nothing on the track stands above about a metre.

**"Clear night right after rain" is also a lighting brief for stage 4:** clear sky and moon, everything wet and reflective, puddles that mirror the forms, and likely ground mist.

**Stage 3 walk-through, 2026-10-13 (100 m proof segment).** About 6,000 instances, 33.7M instanced polygons drawn, Blender at 1.9 GB of memory. A 960×540 still at 16 samples takes ~14.5 s, in either aspect ratio; it took under 1 s before the trees went in. Vertical holds both walls and both ruts at all four check points. Roots crossing the path were offered (procedural) and not built — Nick: "this looks great as is."

**Stage 3 signed off 2026-10-13.**

**Stage 4 lighting brief (Nick, 2026-10-13).** The moon is **ahead of us and off to one side** — backlight: silhouetted trunks, wet ruts glinting toward the camera, the near side left dark for the forms · **very dark** with no forms lit: silhouettes and water glints only · moonlight is a **muted, slightly cool grey**, not cinema blue; the brand grade happens later in Resolve · **low mist in the trench**; visible moon shafts were not asked for · **stars are visible** in the sky gaps and in the puddles — "we are deep in the forest away from cities." The moon itself is never in frame (carried from the course).

**Full moon (Nick, 2026-10-13, on seeing the first night pass: "i want full moon brightness").** This revises "very dark" in the brief above: the moon is a bright full moon with clear moonlit patches where it breaks through the canopy, and the forest is readable under it. The moon stays a directional light, not an HDRI — it needs a steerable direction and crisp shadows.

**Moonlight approved 2026-10-13 ("this looks good").** Moon energy 3.8 (it was 10 at "full moon", lowered once the clearings let light in), azimuth 32° right of ahead, elevation 40°; sky glow (0.08, 0.105, 0.165) with stars. Light reaches the trench through **clearings in the canopy** — four in the 100 m — chosen by Nick over switching tree shadows off: the walk alternates dark stretches and moon pools. Foliage casts solid shadows. Average frame brightness runs about 10 (dark stretch) to 21 (in a pool) of 255.

**Mist, and midnight (Nick, 2026-10-13).** The mist is in the trench **and through the forest** (his suggestion: "making it exist in more of the frame"), layered, wispy, neutral silver, slowly drifting. With it in, the scene read as "early morning", so the target was restated: **it is midnight** — deep blacks, small bright moon pools, a dark sky with stars. Current values: moon 3.0, sky glow (0.028, 0.037, 0.058). Dark stretches now average 6–8 of 255 with 63–75% of the frame near black; a moon pool frame averages about 24.

**Stage 4 check, 2026-10-13.** Both shapes hold at all four check points; the vertical frame carries more sky and stars. Review stills (960×540, 16 samples): 3.2 s each. **One 1920×1080 frame at 64 samples: 11.7 s** — the first near-final number: about 51 hours for 15,840 frames in one shape, before the forms and their lights are added. Wet film on leaves was cut back after Nick flagged "a lot of small glints".

**Stage 4 signed off 2026-10-13.** Final-quality frame (1920×1080, 32 samples): about 5–6 s on the 100 m segment, after a pass that cut it from 11.6 s with no visible change — roughly 24 hours per shape for 15,840 frames, before forms and lights.

## 2.5 The arc across the three tracks (Nick, 2026-10-13)

**The rule above everything: it stays real.** "i do want this to maintain realism. the only not real part should be the forms." Every change along the lane needs a physical reason a forest could have.

**Tempo and event map (Nick's timings; total 10:54 = 654 s = 15,696 frames at 24 fps — the course's 11:00 / 15,840 is superseded).** Track boundaries were not given; only the events below.

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | ERNSTFALL starts, 140 BPM |
| 0:41 | ERNSTFALL drop 1 (tempo unchanged) |
| 1:43 → 2:22 | Tempo rises 140 → 174; drop 2 lands at 2:22 |
| 3:09 → 3:34 | Tempo falls 174 → 140 |
| 4:56 | ABGRUND drop 1 |
| 5:24 → 6:19 | ABGRUND breakdown |
| 7:00 → 7:42 | Tempo falls 140 → 128 (ABGRUND outro), into a rolling wub that runs through all of GRUND |
| 8:13 | GRUND drop 1 |
| 10:12 → 10:54 | Tempo falls 128 → 64; end |

**By track:**
- **ERNSTFALL — the only real modulation is camera speed.** "that is the big change in that song." The lane itself does not change through ERNSTFALL. We start **already inside** the lane.
- **ABGRUND — the moonlight falls away and the forms are the only light.** No moon at all. The cut keeps deepening; whether the floor slopes or the walls rise is Claude's call ("whatever works better") — walls rise, which keeps the standing water flat.
- **ABGRUND → GRUND — the seam is the tempo drop** (140 → 128) into the rolling wub. A sheet of standing water across the floor was proposed and **not accepted** ("im not sold on the water idea but its close") — the realism rule is the objection.
- **GRUND — the floor.** Level; the walls lower, the canopy opens, the moon returns.
- **The end — the clearing is modest:** "just a slight opening to a circle of trees and tall grass but with the moon and stars visible."

**Track boundaries (Nick): ABGRUND starts 4:01, GRUND starts 7:43.** So ERNSTFALL is 0:00–4:01, ABGRUND 4:01–7:43 (its outro is the slowdown), GRUND 7:43–10:54. The moon is lost at 4:01.

**The seam is the valley bottom (accepted).** The lane reaches level ground: banks pull back, the floor widens and flattens, the ground goes soft and the ruts spread into shallow pools. Water with a reason to be there.

**The forms are always there (Nick): "present from the start. they are always there. they are the code sneaking through and then we will have song elements light them up."** This replaces the earlier proposal of one form at a time igniting ahead. Forms exist unlit through the whole lane; the music lights them. It is the public explainer from VISUAL_RESET §2.5 made literal — the wireframe is the code underneath, showing through. ⚠ Not yet reconciled with that file's "one focal form, never a pile" rule, and not yet decided whether a form is a separate object standing in the lane or the world's own geometry showing as wireframe.

**Camera speed follows the felt pulse (Nick, decided).** Dubstep at 140 is felt in half-time (~70); drum and bass at 174 is felt at full time — about 2.5× faster, though the BPM number rises only 24%. So the 174 section runs at about 2.5× walking pace (~3.4 m/s), and the lane is about **1,020 m**, not 884. The camera still gets one dial for the strength of the mapping so it can be judged against the music.

**What a form is in this piece (Nick, decided): the world's own wireframe.** "the second is the code sneaking through. it is exaggerated on the album cover on purpose, but here it should just be the wireframes that light up." Not separate objects standing in the lane: the real forest's geometry showing as wireframe. The cover's free-standing object is a deliberate exaggeration of the same idea, so the cover and the visualizer are two strengths of one mechanic. This is a mode [[working/WOBAR_VISUAL_RESET]] does not yet describe — its §2.5 composite assumes one object, and its "one focal form, never a pile" rule was written for that.

**They light only on kick and snare (Nick, decided):** "they will only light momentarily on kick and snare hits." Momentary flashes, not sustained glow. This replaces "MIDI for ignitions, audio bands for continuous breathing" from §1.

**Distance, if camera speed is strictly proportional to tempo (1.37 m/s at 140 BPM):** 174 → 1.70 m/s, 128 → 1.25, 64 → 0.63. About 320 m by 3:34, 602 m by 7:00, 657 m by 7:42, 845 m by 10:12, **≈ 884 m in total.** ⚠ 140 → 174 is only a 24% speed-up, which may not read as "the big change"; whether to exaggerate the mapping is open.

**How the wireframes behave (Nick, 2026-10-13).**
- **Kick lights the ground** — banks, roots, rocks, the low heavy things. **Snare lights the trees.**
- **Patches, not everything:** the wireframe shows only where the surface is "thin", so it stays *sneaking through*.
- **One colour for the whole piece: the cover's pale teal** ("perfect throughout the whole thing").
- **A slow fade after each hit, on a knob**, tuned so the lane is "mostly dark before the next hit".
- **The strength of the light must be automatable over time.** The ABGRUND breakdown (5:24–6:19) stays dark — "this part of the song feels like falling" — but it does have drums, lighter and sparser, so hits there are weaker, not absent.
- Consequence accepted: with no moon, ABGRUND is seen only in glimpses.

**Revised on seeing the first cages (Nick, 2026-10-13): "overall this is too busy."** The ground carries **no** wireframe — the kick lights **ferns and rocks** instead ("instead of the ground having the wireframe. lets have the ferns and rocks"). And **far fewer things show it**: on the 100 m segment, 35 of 211 firs, 125 of ~2,000 ferns, 27 rocks, clustered in patches. Restraint is the point; a full wireframe forest is wrong.

Build intent (Claude): wireframes are simplified low-poly cages around the real things, not the scans' dense meshes — a fir's needle cards would light as a blur, and low-poly is the blueprint. Flashes are softened in the 174 section, where kick and snare together approach three per second.
