## §1 — Ranking mechanics

| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | The three signals that matter most for Reels are **watch time**, **sends per reach**, **likes per reach** — stated by Mosseri, Jan 2025 | **VERIFIED** — convergent across many independent T2 restatements | T2 (Later, Sprout Social, Hootsuite, Dataslayer, Socialync) |
| 1.2 | **Sends per reach (DM shares) is the strongest signal for reaching non-followers**; likes per reach matters more for existing followers | **VERIFIED** — consistent across all sources | T2 |
| 1.3 | Sends are weighted "3–5×" a like | **PARTIAL** — direction verified, the multiplier is unsourced folklore. Do not build on the number | T3 |
| 1.4 | Watch time is decided in the **first 3 seconds** — continuation past 3s is the gate | **VERIFIED** (direction), exact weighting unpublished | T2 |
| 1.5 | Instagram runs **separate ranking systems per surface** (Feed / Reels / Stories / Explore), each weighting signals differently; Meta dropped "the algorithm" as a singular term in 2025 | **VERIFIED** | T2, consistent with Meta's own language |
| 1.6 | "Your Algorithm" — a user-facing dashboard letting viewers add/remove topic categories — went global early 2026 | **PARTIAL** — reported consistently, not independently confirmed T1 this session | T2 |

**Read for Wobar:** the account's biggest post got **316 sends on 11,463 views (2.8% sends-per-reach)** — an unusually strong signal on the metric that matters most. Distribution is not the constraint. That post still returned **0 follows**. The system did its job; the account failed to convert.

### 4.1 — The category hypothesis is REFUTED
**"Abstract visual content can't grow on Instagram" is false.** `elekktronaut` is 45K followers on nothing but faceless abstract generative art, pulling 4.9K–14.4K views per Reel (11–32% of follower count). Wobar's visualizers pull 133–314.

The gap is not the category. Visible differences worth testing:
- **His work is high-contrast and often on bright grounds** (orange, hot pink, electric blue). Wobar's visualizers are dark-on-black — near-invisible as a thumbnail in a bright feed on a phone.
- **One legible form per frame**, readable at thumbnail scale. Wobar's are full-frame texture fields.
- **438 posts in one consistent style** = a recognizable format, not a gallery of one-offs.

### 4.2 — Curation beats volume, hard
Of The Trees wiped his Instagram in January 2026 and rebuilt it as a named world — "Moonglade Park," in-world highlight reels (MUSIC / TOUR / CAMP / LABEL / FAN PAGE / BLAAP), a "PARK SERVICE" identity. **25 posts, 160K followers, 37–66% views-per-follower.** Shlump: **1,658 posts, 6–13%.** Roughly a 5× reach-efficiency gap in favor of the curated era account.

This matters twice over: Of The Trees runs **Memory Palace**, the crown target in [[working/WOBAR_PLACEMENT_PLAN]]. The person Wobar most wants to be seen by just demonstrated, on his own account, that he values a curated world over a content feed. And an **era** is exactly the mechanic WOBAR_WORLD flagged as "parked."

### 4.3 — Face is not the variable
Shlump is the most face-forward comp and has the *worst* reach efficiency of the four. Of The Trees is largely faceless/silhouetted and has the best. elekktronaut is fully faceless at 11–32%. **Face-vs-faceless does not predict performance in this sample. Consistency of world does.**

### 4.4 — Even at scale, the human beat wins the tail
INZO's two biggest posts in the sample are a merch shot (209K) and a moment with a kid at a show (148K) — above every stage/laser shot. Human moments outperform spectacle *within* an otherwise visual account. That is a mix argument, not a replacement argument.

## §5 — What this changes

1. **Stop treating the visualizer underperformance as a brand problem.** It is a legibility-and-format problem, and `elekktronaut` is the existence proof. Dark-on-black one-offs are the specific failure, not abstraction.
2. **The platform is actively rewarding serialization** (Series, June 2026) and gates the feature on already serializing. Serialize on plain Reels now.
3. **Trial Reels and Series are both locked at Wobar's size.** 1,000 followers is a real functional threshold, not a vanity number — it unlocks the safe-testing instrument. That argues for one narrow push to 1K.
4. **Curation is a lever Wobar hasn't pulled.** 101 posts of mixed identity is the Shlump end of the spectrum with none of the volume payoff. The Of The Trees move — wipe/archive, rebuild as a named era — is available, brand-native, and endorsed by the exact person Wobar wants to reach.
5. **Sends are not the problem; the profile is.** 2.8% sends-per-reach on the top post is strong. The failure is everything after the swipe.
