# Format account templates (2026-11-17)

**Question:** which 3 to 5 templates should Nick's format accounts launch with? **Score:** views only.
**Read:** 15 TikTok video searches (360 videos, 274 accounts) plus 5 targeted searches; 45 TikTok template accounts reach-read (followers, last 12 non-pinned views); 12 Instagram repost/format pages reach-read; grids read by eye for 8. Notes: data/template_notes.md. Numbers: data/tt_format_reach.json, raw/ig_format_reach.json.

## The headline
**Templates break the follower ceiling, on TikTok.** Artist accounts sit at about 0.37 views per follower (median views of recent posts divided by followers). The best TikTok template pages sit at 1.2 to 11:

On Instagram the same kind of page does not break out: ambnt.prod is 12.4k followers there (102k on TikTok) at about 0.95; the lane's repost pages (deeptempo140, riddimnyc, northern_invasion, crucast_, clubstudiotime) sit at 0.2 to 0.7, steady 10k to 28k per post. **TikTok is where a template account grows from zero; Instagram is where a curation page earns steady, collab-driven reach.**

## The warning
**Most template pages are lottery tickets.** Of 37 accounts with enough posts, only 12 had 10 or more of their last 12 over 10k. Many have one giant pinned hit and recent posts in the hundreds: earphones.heaven (50.5M pinned, recent median 4.2k), dubstepfbi on TikTok (14M pinned, 1k), 8hmusic (7.1M, 7.2k), midcaredits101 (8.7M, 5.3k), a numbered "Pt 1169" series (1.4M pinned, 3.3k). The template gets you into the draw; the pages that stay consistent add a **utility hook** (it is useful to the viewer: "Designed For Edits", "Artist ID", "headphones on") and post on rhythm.

## Templates for WOBAR, ranked by fit and evidence

### 1. Rap flip, DAW screen, one quoted line (the ambnt.prod model). Strongest fit.
- **Recipe:** screen recording of the session (piano roll / arrangement), no face. One white line in quotes, centred: '"[famous rap song]" But It's 140' / 'But It's Cinematic' / 'Designed For Edits'. 15 to 30 seconds, drop near the start.
- **Evidence:** 12/12 over 10k, median 128k on TikTok; 11M on '"Love Me" But It's Designed For Edits'. UK 140 scene does the same with freestyles (Vibe Chemistry and Lupole flipping Elro and Giggs; Lupole pinned 1.7M; an Elro mash-up 90.5k).
- **Why it works where plain flips failed in the artist pass:** the second hook is the utility frame ("Designed For Edits", "Cinematic"): the post is a tool for editors, so it spreads as a sound, not only as a video.
- **Effort:** very low. **Scale:** one per flip; the catalogue of rap songs is endless. **Lane:** the lane correction says rap flips are the center of mass.

### 2. Dark void ID page (the fuzzythingy model). Closest to vector-void.
- **Recipe:** grainy black-and-white loop of a lone figure or object in a black void, glitch, no text on the frame. Caption: "Artist ID: [artist] - [track] (Out Now / Unreleased)". Posts Nick's own IDs mixed with other underground bass IDs, crediting them.
- **Evidence:** 10/12, median 28.8k on 15.5k followers (1.9 per follower); pinned 659k.
- **Effort:** low once a bank of void loops exists (Blender renders already in progress). **Scale:** high; every ID is a post, and crediting other artists invites their reposts. **Fit:** the world without explaining it; the "ID" is the voice line.

### 3. Black-and-white archive edit (the monochromedits model).
- **Recipe:** B&W documentary / interview / film footage, one small serif line centred, dnb / ukg / 140 audio. Bio links a playlist.
- **Evidence:** 10/12, median 33k on 19k (1.7); pinned posts 582k to 1M.
- **Effort:** medium (sourcing footage). **Risk:** footage rights; use public-domain archive (see reference/WOBAR_ARCHIVE.md). **Fit:** restraint, muted grade, no face.

### 4. Night footage + one line (the woosh_mei model).
- **Recipe:** grainy low-light phone clips (trains, rain, empty stations), one line on screen, track in caption. woosh_mei uses lyrics; WOBAR would use one line in the light register, never doctrine.
- **Evidence:** 11/12, median 135k on 181k (0.75).
- **Effort:** low (Nick's own phone footage). **Fit:** high on mood; check that it does not drift toward sad-girl lyric pages.

### 5. Audio-first, near-black frame (the bassboosted.music_ model).
- **Recipe:** almost-black frame (a thin waveform or a single point of light), "headphones on" cue, the drop is the content.
- **Evidence:** 12/12, median 840k on 1.1M; the "headphones" family (hxlivibez 1.7M followers, bluevibelyrics) is huge but runs low views per follower (0.04 to 0.3).
- **Fit:** the void is literal here. **Risk:** the "bass boosted" genre reads low-brow; keep the frame designed, not a phone screen.

### Not recommended for WOBAR, but noted
- **Edit-culture soundtrack pages** (anime, Kanye, military, car edits) have the highest views per follower of anything read (5 to 11) but are figurative fandom content, the cheese vector. The WOBAR-compatible route into that engine is template 1: make tracks **for** editors ("Designed For Edits") instead of making the edits.
- **Instagram repost / curation page** (deeptempo140, riddimnyc, northern_invasion): steady 10k to 15k per post at 0.4 to 0.7 per follower, built on collab posts with the artists. A sensible later Instagram account, not a launch template.

## Suggested launch set
TikTok first: **1 (rap flip, DAW)** and **2 (void ID page)**, with **4 (night footage)** as the third if there is capacity. Each account one template only, posting on a fixed rhythm, no cross-boosting between accounts. Judge each on its 10k+ count and median over the first 30 posts, not on any single post.

## Limits
- One snapshot. Search results favour what is already popular, so this oversamples winners; the lottery-page share is a reminder of how many fail.
- TikTok grid counts are views; Instagram grid counts are plays. Comparable within a platform, not across.
- Templates were read from covers and captions, not watched with audio.
- The Instagram owner read of 461 hashtag posts was still running at write time and is not in this file.
