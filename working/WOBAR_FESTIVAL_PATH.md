## §1 — THE BINDING CONSTRAINT, AND IT IS NOT SIZE

**Nick has played zero rooms he did not book himself.** Flow State only, confirmed 2026-08-15.

The original framing of this work was *"how big of an artist do I need to be to play that festival."* **That is the wrong axis, and the evidence is first-party.** Bass Coast — one of the few festivals in the lane that publishes its curation criteria — names what it actually screens on:

> - "Evidence of proven performance experience"
> - "Recent history performing at the venue"

*Source: Bass Coast, "First Timer's Guide to Applying to Play Festivals," loaded direct 2026-08-15.*

Neither is a follower count. **The field Wobar fails is performance history, and no amount of streaming growth fills it.** 22 monthly listeners is not what disqualifies the application; an empty past-performance line is.

**This inverts the priority.** Growing the online numbers does not open festival doors. **One external booking does more for this path than a year of listener growth**, because it converts a blank field into a non-blank one. Everything downstream is a repeat of that move.

> ⚠ **The corollary, stated so it is not lost:** this path and the online goal are **not the same work**, and progress on one does not produce progress on the other. [[working/WOBAR_ROADMAP]] §1 holds them as one goal with two expressions — that remains true of the *goal*, and is false of the *mechanism*.

## §2 — THE FIVE ENTRY MECHANISMS

**Replaces the size axis.** A festival is not reached by being big enough; it is reached through exactly one of five mechanisms, and which one it is determines everything about whether Nick can act.

| # | Mechanism | Gate | Reachable at 0 gigs? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Open competition / contest** | A track. Nothing else. | ✅ **YES — the only one** |
| **2** | **Open application window** | Proven performance history + scene involvement | ❌ Open in form, closed in fact |
| **3** | **Label showcase stage** | A release on that label | ❌ — but the label door is separately reachable |
| **4** | **Agency roster** | An agent, which requires draw | ❌ Years out |
| **5** | **Promoter relationship** | Someone in the room vouches | ⚠️ **Excluded here by decision** — this is the Flow State lever, [[working/WOBAR_BOOKING_ROADMAP]] |

**Mechanism 1 is the entire near-term surface of this file.** Everything else is a ladder (§5).

**Why the taxonomy and not a size ranking:** the vault has already found this shape twice and both times it was the finding. [[working/WOBAR_BOOKING_ROADMAP]] §2 — the two densest label clusters are shut, so the centre is entered by relationship not submission. §5.5 — *"the centre of this scene is entered through rooms, not inboxes."* **A festival lineup is that same graph.** Sorting festivals by size would have reproduced the ranking error §5.5's orbit layer already paid for.

## §5 — THE RUNG LADDER

**Replaces "how big do I need to be."** Each rung's gate is a *thing*, not a number, and the rung below it is what produces the thing.

| Rung | What it is | Gate | Wobar |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Rooms you book yourself | — | ⬅️ **HERE.** Flow State only |
| **1** | **External local support slot, SLC** | A promoter who is not Nick says yes | The next move |
| **2** | Regional club support / small-festival undercard | A draw number, or local reputation | — |
| **3** | Small + transformational festival undercard | **Proven performance history** — Bass Coast says so in writing | — |
| **4** | Mid-tier side stage / label showcase | A release on the right label | — |
| **5** | Billed slot, major festival | Agency roster + draw | — |

**Rung 1 is the whole game right now.** It is the only rung whose gate Nick can attack directly, and it is the rung that converts the empty past-performance field. The venue map already exists and was built for a different purpose — [[working/WOBAR_BOOKING_ROADMAP]] §5.6: **Boxpac Project · The Complex / Rockwell · Soundwell · Fear Factory SLC · The Great Saltair (Magna) · Granary Live · Utah State Fairpark.** Those are the rooms and promoters booking Wobar's exact tier in Wobar's own city.

### 🔴 THE BYPASS — and it is why mechanism 1 outranks everything

**The Discovery Project skips rungs 1 through 4 in a single move.** A winner goes from no booking history to a stage at EDC or Electric Forest, judged on a track, with no performance history requested. SLANDER, NGHTMRE and Sullivan King came up through it. 🟡

**It is the only rung-skipping door found anywhere in this landscape, and entering costs a track Nick has already made.** Losing costs nothing and leaves no mark. That asymmetry is the entire argument.

**But it is a lottery, not a plan.** The ladder is the plan; the bypass is a free ticket bought every cycle while the ladder is climbed. **Do not let the bypass become the strategy** — that is exactly the failure [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §7 names: *does it cause an uncomfortable action, or license a comfortable one?* Entering contests is comfortable. Asking an SLC promoter for a slot is not.

## §7 — STANDING RAILS

- **Keep this path separate from Flow State.** Nick's call. Even where doors converge, the files stay apart — the point is a path that works if the promoter seat goes away.
- **Contest entries cost a track and nothing else.** No exclusivity trap has been checked. ⚠️ **Read the terms before entering any remix contest** — some assign rights.
- **Application windows are short and annual.** Bass Coast's is seven days. **A missed window is a full year.** Whatever tracks these dates must be outside the vault — the vault holds no calendars ([[WOBAR_CONTEXT]] Drift Rule §1).
- **Autumn is when summer festivals book.** Applications and pitches go out in autumn and winter; by spring the bills are full. 🟡
- **A festival list is a comfortable action.** It must terminate in *contact this human by this date* or it licenses research in place of a booking.

### 9.3 — Shambhala: the method returns null, and the reason is the finding

**There is no smaller counterpart because there is no portfolio.** One event, one family, one farm, no parent company, no sibling festivals, no door. **The parent-org method works on four of the five and returns nothing here.**

> **And the property that makes it unreachable is the property Nick likes about it.** Fierce independence from corporate structure *is* the absence of a system, and a system is what a door is. **This is §0's size/reachability inversion confirmed on Nick's own list** — the most corporate name on it (EDC) is the most enterable, and the most independent one (Shambhala) is a closed room.

**That is the VA compilation economy [[working/RESEARCH_LABEL_LANDSCAPE]] §2.6 already named the biggest strategic find in that file** — *a 29-slot compilation vs a one-slot signing.* Subsidia is the same shape, attached to a dream festival, and the vault has never listed it. **Demo route unverified — check `subsidia.ca`.** 🔴
