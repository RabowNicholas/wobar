# IG PEER COMP SWEEP v2 — LANE C

## What changed from v1
v1 sampled n=4 on **whose handle was resolvable by web search** — which correlates with press and management. Every account over-indexed and no flatliner appeared, exactly as that bias predicts.

v2 draws from six label rosters (Beatport), resolves handles from each **label's own Instagram following list**, and sweeps regardless of outcome. **Two of v1's findings do not survive.** See §4.

## §1 — The flatliners exist. The lane's floor is not high.
v1 concluded every peer over-indexes. False — that was the sampling.

**Views ÷ followers ranges 22% to 500%.** The bottom of the band: gunpointmusic_ 22%, itsokayjake 28%, secret_stvsh 29%, iseeusound 30%. `@iseeusound` has 5,638 followers and a post at **819 views**. `@vuloramusic` has a post at **694**.

## §2 — CORRECTION: the "16×/30× content gap" in v1 was an artifact
v1 compared Wobar's raw view counts against peers with 10–50× the follower base and read the difference as a content-quality gap. That comparison was not normalized and the framing was wrong.

**Normalized, Wobar's visualizer posts sit at ~51% views-per-follower — inside the peer band, above four of the fifteen accounts swept.**

**The real deficit is the follower base: 400 against a peer median around 5,000.** That is a different problem with a different fix. Content is not performing catastrophically per-follower; there are simply almost no followers, and nothing in the current mix converts reach into them (706 profile visits → ~0 follows, per Wobar's own Insights).

## §3 — CORRECTION: "face-to-camera is the mechanic" does not survive
v1's cohort was four face-forward accounts, so face looked like the variable. With a neutral frame it isn't.

- **@coido__** — Berlin, 4,360 followers, posts **hardware jams and abstract 3D chrome loops**, no face. Those abstract loops pull 4.2K–7.9K each — **~124% of followers.**
- **@vanda.wav** — 1,724 followers, **DAW session screens, artwork, moody typography**, separate art page `@vanda_visuals`. ~145% of followers.

Both beat the face-forward large accounts on reach efficiency (itsokayjake 28%, gunpointmusic_ 22%, secret_stvsh 29%).

**Abstract, faceless, production-forward content is a fully viable baseline in this lane.** The brand position is not the constraint.

## §4 — What DOES hold, across all 15
1. **Face + claim + recognizable flip is the ceiling mechanic.** Every single account's top post is that shape: flozone's Hoobastank flip 90.7K · gunpoint's Denzel Curry reload 41.4K · dêtre's "140 Kids at shows" 266K · cozykev's John Summit remix 25.6K.
2. **Static track artwork is the floor in nearly every account** — weston_luhv 2,106 · gunpointmusic_ 2,357 · spukemusic 2,315 · detrebeats 2,506 · itsokayjake 4,315. In every case the account's lowest recent post.
3. **Wobar's visualizers are behaving like artwork posts, not like COIDO's loops.** COIDO's work is motion-forward, high-contrast, one legible form. Wobar's are dark-on-black texture fields. That is the specific, fixable difference — consistent with the `@elekktronaut` read in RESEARCH_IG_MECHANICS §4.1.
4. **Almost nobody runs a named recurring series.** Confirmed across all 15. Combined with Meta's Series push (June 2026), still an unoccupied position.
5. **DM-trigger formats are in active use** — `@acyanmusic` "REPLY OR DM: ONETWO", Good Society's "text us for invites", Flow State's "text hangar". Sends/DMs are the top-weighted ranking signal (RESEARCH_IG_MECHANICS §1.2), and this lane already exploits it.
6. **Korum Management** appears on five accounts (itsokayjake, cozykev__, flozonebass, gunpointmusic_, secret_stvsh) — real, but clustered in the Wubaholics/Atlanta orbit, not universal. v1 overstated it; the neutral frame surfaced plenty of non-Korum artists (coido, vanda, vulora, iseeu, spüke, acyan, detre→Warpath).

## §5 — The closest structural comp to Wobar
**@vanda.wav — 102 posts (Wobar: 101), 1,724 followers, faceless, DAW/artwork/visual-forward, separate visuals account.** Same output volume, same aesthetic register, ~4× the followers and ~12× the median views.

That is the realistic near-term target shape — not flozonebass at 24.5K.

**@eatermusic** is worth noting separately: 12.1K followers on **4 posts**, zero following, "expect the unexpected." A wiped/reset account, same move as Of The Trees. Evidence the reset play works in this lane too.

## §6 — What this means for Wobar
1. **Stop treating the visualizers as the problem.** Per-follower they're mid-band. Fix the legibility (contrast, one form, motion) and they're a viable baseline — COIDO and vanda prove it at this exact scale.
2. **The bottleneck is follower acquisition, not reach per post.** 9,148 non-followers reached in 90 days produced ~0 follows. Nothing in the current mix asks for or earns the follow.
3. **The ceiling mechanic (face + claim + flip) is available and unused.** Wobar has the flips — Down Bad, World-A-Reggae. Whether the face is required is now an open question rather than a settled one, because coido/vanda get baseline reach without it. What is NOT optional is **the claim** — a stated reason to watch, in the first frame.
4. **Kill the static-artwork post format.** It is the measured floor in every account in the lane.

## §7 — Limits
- n=15 of a ~72 frame; 56 handles resolved, 12 supplied by Nick, 3 unresolved (Daffy, Kells, Clifford — all PullUp).
- Accounts prioritized toward the **low-follower end** after the first six, deliberately, because that part of the distribution was unmeasured. Stated rather than hidden; it biases the sample *against* the over-index effect, which is the conservative direction.
- Public view counts only — no retention, saves, sends, or follows-per-post. Those need mobile Insights and are still the highest-value missing number.
- `@hamshyre` is India-based; 390% is not a US benchmark.
- Posting dates not captured.
