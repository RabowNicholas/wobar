# WHAT TO MAKE NEXT
*12 Aug · from 10 posts, 6 mature*

## MAKE
**Another studio "samples i'll never get cleared".**

Best converter on the account and it isn't close — **15 follows off 2,811 viewers**, and 002 repeated it at 12. It shares *and* converts. Nothing else you make does both.

## KEEP
**The GIF / front-camera comment posts.** They are not underperforming — they are doing a different job and they are the best you have at it.

**73 and 35 comments; a 3.14% comment rate against 0.09–0.68% on the music posts.** Instagram names comments as one of its three metrics. These are the top of the funnel.

## STOP
**Standalone talking-head advice.**

*"going viral is bad for your music"* — 605 viewers, **0 shares, 0 comments, 0 follows, 0 profile taps.** The only post that produced literally nothing. One post, so a strong lead rather than a proven rule — but nothing sits on the other side.

## CHECK — once, ten minutes
**Read the comments on the two GIF posts and write down who they are.**

108 comments, mostly non-followers. If they are lane producers, that format is the funnel working exactly as designed and you should feed it. If they are randoms, it is noise that looks like engagement. **Nobody has ever looked**, and [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §4 ranks not looking as the single likeliest way this whole thing dies.

## WATCH
**Shares** — the metric that separates content types, and the one Instagram distributes on. Music **331 · 25 · 19 · 16 · 10** against commentary **3 · 0 · 0 · 0 · 0**, ten posts, no overlap.

**Comments** — the metric that separates the *other* direction. Do not average the two together; they measure different jobs.

## NOT YET
**That recognisable source material is the real variable.** SpongeBob/wumbo and World-A-Reggae are both flips of something the listener already knows, and both are moving. That cuts across the music/commentary split and would be more useful than it — it tells you *what to flip*, not just what kind of post to make. **The wumbo posts are still inside the maturity window; they cannot be read yet.** Fill `track` in the log and this resolves next cycle.

**That memes are worthless.** See KEEP. The open question is not whether they engage — it's whether the engagement is the right people, which is what CHECK answers.

## STANDING — check before posting
- **Under 3:00**, or Instagram won't recommend it to non-followers.
- **Never upload a TikTok export.** Third-party watermarks block recommendation. Post the clean master to both.
- **Hook in 3 seconds**, and assume sound off — the text carries the hook, the sound does the work after.
- **Music first.** This never moves ahead of a release.
