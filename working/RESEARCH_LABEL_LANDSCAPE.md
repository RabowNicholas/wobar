# LABEL LANDSCAPE — DARK/WONKY 140

> **Lane locked 2026-07-26:** dark/wonky 140 + rap-flip adjacency, US-centric. Originals contain no uncleared samples. Denver de-weighted (the goal is online, not the room). International deep-dubstep treated as appendix.

## ⚠ Data quality — read before acting

Run: 11 parallel research agents, 2026-07-26. **The session-wide web search budget (200 calls) was exhausted mid-run.** Consequences that travel with every claim below:

1. **Beatport returned HTTP 403 on every page, for every agent.** No chart positions, no per-label chart presence, and no release-count verification anywhere in this document.
2. **No signing floors were obtained.** SoundCloud profile fetches failed across the board. The "what size artist do they sign at" field — one of the three things this sweep was built to answer — **is entirely missing.**
3. **LabelRadar portals render blank to a fetch** (JS-rendered, login-walled). Where a portal is listed as "open," that means the URL resolved, **not** that anyone read the form.
4. Two agents returned near-empty. Chart-frequency discovery achieved ~2–3% coverage. **The "labels that don't advertise" discovery pass did not run at all** — it refused rather than guess, which was correct.

**What this means:** the tier table below is built on door type, roster composition and catalogue evidence. It is **not** built on chart data or signing-floor data. Treat tiering as a considered judgment, not a measurement.

## §1 — THE TIER TABLE

Tiers are defined by **how you get in**, not by label size.

- `T1` — open door, in-lane, demonstrably signs unknowns → **submit**
- `T2` — open door, but weaker fit or a higher roster floor → **submit later, or with the right track**
- `T3` — no public door, or explicitly closed → **not submittable; watch or earn**
- `WRONG LANE` — real labels, real doors, wrong music → **stop spending effort here**

## §2 — THE STRUCTURAL READ

**Four of the seven doors currently in `WOBAR_GROWTH_PLAN.md` are aimed at the wrong lane.** Gravitas, Local Void, Trippy Bee and SubCarbon were all selected while the vault believed the comps were Of The Trees / Shlump / INZO. That's the outro register — 24% of the set. The door list never got re-aimed after the lane correction.

**The in-lane doors cluster tightly.** Wubaholics, Jadū Dala, Deadbeats and Bassrush between them carry STVSH, dêtre, Gunpoint, Phrva, Tape B, Blurrd Vzn, Quackson, OkayJake — nearly the whole reference set. It's a small world, and those labels are visible to each other. One credential inside that cluster is legible to all of them.

**Deadbeats is not aspirational.** Their stated A&R posture, from the UKF label spotlight: *"not concerned with artist size or social growth, focuses on track/release and if audience will respond."* Combined with an open LabelRadar door, that moves it out of T4 entirely. The old `DROPPED — too broad/mainstream` verdict in the Placement Plan is dead twice over.

**The best-fit door in the whole sweep is closed.** Wormhole Music Group is the cleanest dark/wonky-140 match found anywhere — and it is shut, with only a paid Patreon workaround. Worth a standing watch.

## §3 — THE LIVESTREAM A&R DOOR

**The most valuable finding of this sweep, and it was absent from the vault entirely.**

**Space Yacht — "Tune Reactor."** Live A&R on Twitch, **Mon / Wed / Fri, 4PM PT**, 300+ episodes. Artists submit demos for live feedback and on-the-spot signing. Nominal donation per submission.

> **"About 80 percent of what the label has signed so far has come from Tune Reactor submissions."**

Quackson and Blurrd Vzn are on the roster.

**Cyclops Recordings (Subtronics) runs the same shape** — CyclopsArmyTV, weekly, demos submitted on stream. Gunpoint on roster.

**Why this beats a demo inbox at Wobar's stage:**

| Demo inbox | Livestream A&R |
|---|---|
| Silence is the modal response | Feedback in minutes, out loud |
| 60-day exit clock per track | Repeatable 3×/week |
| Burns a reserve slot | Feedback ≠ submission — costs no exclusivity |
| No information on *why* | You hear the rejection reasoning, on every track, including other people's |

That last row is the real prize. **This is the only channel found anywhere that returns information about the taste bar** — which the Growth Plan names as the actual binding constraint and the one thing no research can address. Watching an A&R react to 40 tracks a week is the cheapest calibration instrument available.

2. **Memory Palace has a submissions page and it says closed.** The vault records "no portal; relationship/scene entry only." Correct status: a page exists, and it reads *"(we are not currently accepting demos at this time)."*

3. **Wormhole's disputed status is resolved: CLOSED.** Two prior researchers found it open and closed on the same day. The label's Linktree — its official submissions hub — states *"SUBMISSIONS CURRENTLY CLOSED."*

4. **Deep Dark & Dangerous's demo email could not be confirmed.** The vault lists `demo@deepdarkdangerous.com`. This sweep found **only a Bandcamp contact form** and could not locate that address on any label-controlled surface. Either stale or unpublished. **Verify before sending.**

5. **Downgrade two `[SOLID]` claims in `WOBAR_GROWTH_PLAN.md` to `[UNVERIFIED]`:**
   - *"LabelRadar judges a 20-second clip you select"*
   - *"LabelRadar is credit-metered at ~$1/refill"*

   Both are login-walled. **Two independent verification attempts have now failed.** Nobody has actually read the submission flow.

6. **Gravitas's "second undisclosed LabelRadar door" is unconfirmed.** One agent cited a portal URL; the platform sweep could not find Gravitas in LabelRadar's public directory. Possibly unlisted, possibly wrong. Same ambiguity applies to the Wubaholics and Jadū Dala portals — the URLs resolve, but the directory doesn't list them.

**Recurring, worth calendaring:** Bassrush **The Prophecy** (Vol. 13 Jan 2026; dêtre + Gunpoint have both appeared) · Deadbeats **We Are Deadbeats** (Vol. 6) · Dome of Doom **Spring / Summer** comps · Caspa via UKF/Dub Police (closed Jun 2026, watch next cycle) · Space Yacht runs many concurrent series (Big Bass Ting, Tech My House, A Sequence In Time, Critical Mass).

**The structural finding matters more than the listings:** **no centralised bass/140 compilation calendar exists anywhere**, and calls cluster in **winter/early spring**. Late July is a dead window. This is a watch process to build, not a lever to pull now.

**Remix contests** — Remiexs (`remiexs.com/remixcontest`) is the largest aggregator but does not display deadlines on its list view. Currently listed in the 140 space: Vehemence & Externator, E4RC & Hysteric, Al Dubone/Dub Plantage. All lean deathstep/robostep — adjacent to Wobar, not centre.

## §7 — WHAT THIS SWEEP STILL DOES NOT KNOW

1. **Signing floors — for any label.** The core question "am I big enough" is unanswered everywhere. Needs SoundCloud follower counts per roster, which no agent could fetch.
2. **Chart presence — for any label.** Beatport 403'd universally.
3. **Whether the LabelRadar portals for Wubaholics / Jadū Dala / Gravitas actually accept submissions.** URLs resolve; forms were never read.
4. **What any of these labels actually require** — file format, exclusivity terms, response times. Almost none publish it.
5. **The labels that don't advertise.** The discovery pass built to find them never ran. This is the single biggest remaining gap and the one most likely to hold short-queue doors.
6. **The ladder question** — whether T1 credentials actually precede T4 placements, or whether T4 artists entered by collaboration. Requires artist release histories, deliberately out of scope this run.
7. **Whether the music clears the bar.** Unchanged, unaddressed, and still the binding constraint.
