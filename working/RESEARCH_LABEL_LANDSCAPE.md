## §2.6 — THE ARTIST-DISCOGRAPHY SWEEP (2026-07-27) — 🟢 LAYER A

The "labels that don't advertise" pass, finally run. Premise: every prior list was built by **searching for demo pages**, which only finds labels that *market* submissions — the longest queues by definition. This inverts it: walk artist discographies, collect every label, work out the door afterwards.

### Two methodological failures found (and fixed mid-sweep)

**1. Beatport's search page truncates at ~5–6 releases.** A first pass over 16 artists using search results produced mostly "zero new labels" — that verdict is **unreliable**. Proof: **OkayJake** showed **6 releases / 4 labels** on search, and **16 releases / 6 labels** on his artist page. The shallow pass missed Subsidia and Mersiv Sound Music entirely.

**2. Beatport fragments artists across duplicate profiles.** **dêtre** has two entries under the same slug: `detre/1046005` (Wormhole only, 1 release) and `detre/852168` (**13 releases** across PullUp, Good Society ×2, Wubaholics ×2, Odyzey ×2, Bassrush ×3, Wakaan ×3). Neither profile shows the other's catalogue. **Any artist walk must enumerate all profile variants.**

**Correct method:** search → enumerate every artist-profile variant → load `/artist/<slug>/<id>/releases` on each. ~4–6 calls per artist. Note artist pages themselves paginate at 25 — Blurrd Vzn, Quackson and Ero808 all have page 2+ **not yet read**.

**Scope run:** 12 artists deep (STVSH, Gunpoint, Blurrd Vzn, Quackson, Daggz, Ero808, Phrva, OkayJake, Jungle Rootz, ISEEU) + 16 shallow. Artists used **only as a discovery index for labels** — no artist-side output, per scope.
