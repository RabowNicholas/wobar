# BOOKING ROADMAP — FLOW STATE

> **The reframe this rests on.** Nick has a monthly residency and books the bill. **Booking someone means they hear his set, at full length, in a room, without being asked to.** [[working/WOBAR_SURFACES]] rates live at ceiling 3 — deeper than every surface but the music — and [[working/WOBAR_ROADMAP]] §5 calls Flow State *"the only fan-conversion machine in this project."* A DM asks a stranger for four minutes. A booking gives them ninety of yours and pays them for it. **The booking is the strongest version of the outreach, not a parallel track to it.**
>
> **Sequencing rail: book → relationship → send. Never in one breath.** A booking that arrives with a pitch attached reads as transactional and burns the one non-replicable asset in the project.

---

## §1 — THE MECHANISM

Two axes, deliberately not summed into one number. Collapsing stature and reach into a single tier is what made the first pass of [[working/WOBAR_COLLAB_TARGETS]] useless.

**Axis 1 — STRATEGIC VALUE (what the booking buys).** Scored 0–3 each, in the spirit of the two screens that did the real work in [[working/RESEARCH_LABEL_LANDSCAPE]] §1 — cheap, discriminating, near-binary.

| | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DOOR** | none | on a label with an open door | label principal / A&R | **runs their own imprint** |
| **PROXIMITY** | none | shares 1 label with the dream tier | 2 | **3+** |
| **TASTE** | no evidence | same labels as set artists | **collaborates with** a set artist | **in Nick's set** |

**Axis 2 — RUNG (what it costs).** Ranked by measured size and chart presence as a cost proxy. **Fee, capacity, routing and who has already played are Nick's inputs and are not in this file** — the roadmap is built to be independent of them so it stays valid when they change.

**Standing rail:** an artist absent from Beatport is logged **not assessable**, never scored 0. A scraped zero looks like data, which is why it survives review — the error that cost a confident false finding on 2026-08-03.

## §5 — WHAT RAN AND WHAT DIDN'T

**All 17 swept, in two passes on 2026-08-04.** Pass 1: Distinct Motive · Boogie T · Of The Trees · LYNY · INZO · SAKA · Tape B · Chmura · rSUN · EAZYBAKED · En:vy · Yoko · Potions. Pass 2: FLY · Shlump · Zeds Dead · LSDREAM.

> **Pass 1 deprioritised Zeds Dead and LSDREAM as low-value because their labels were already known. Nick overruled it and was right** — their value was never their own discography, it was the compilations they anchor. **We Are Deadbeats Vol. 6 and Trippy Bee Vol. 2 produced more bookable names than any other source in this file** (§3.5). **Recorded as a method lesson: at the top of a scene, the artist is not the unit of information — the compilation is.**

**🔴 FLY — a definitive negative result, not a lookup failure.** His Beatport profile (`/artist/fly/50224`) is a **three-way conflation**: a deep-house producer (Deep Strips, Road Story Records, 2024–26), a Thai rock band on GMM Grammy (1996–2017, dozens of entries), and the bass artist. **The bass FLY has exactly two entries in it** — *Moonglade Park* (Of The Trees, Memory Palace) and HARD Summer 2024 — **both as a guest on someone else's release.** Three further credits exist only via collaborators (*Dash* with LYNY; *Cataclysmic* with SubDocta and Shlump; Bassrush *Prophecy Vol. 2*; Sleeveless Warriors Vol. 2).
→ **He has no independent Beatport catalogue.** This confirms the vault's structural claim directly rather than by inference: uncleared flips cannot be distributed, so the rap-flip register is genuinely invisible here. **FLY is SoundCloud-native by construction and no Beatport-shaped method will ever reach him.**

**Shlump — the strike was about the right thing and it doesn't reach here.** Wakaan is his home (~25 releases), but **he is on Deep Dark & Dangerous six times** (*Alien Technology* + remixes, *Flying Saucer*, *Shaolin Shadowboxing*, *Zero Gravity*, *Pull Up* 2025), plus Deadbeats ×5 and Circus ×4. **The artist [[reference/WOBAR_SONIC]] struck as a psy-bass comp is deep inside the deep-dubstep label** — he is a genuine cross-lane bridge, which is a separate fact from whether he describes Wobar's sound. Nick's instinct to keep him on the dream tier is supported.

**⚠ Potions is not assessable by this method.** Nine Beatport releases on experimental/house imprints (100% Silk, Hausu Mountain, True Mother) with one bass connection (*Zoom In* with tiedye ky). Either a different artist or SoundCloud-native. **Logged as not assessable, not scored 0.**

**Mega-compilations were excluded from PROXIMITY.** EDC Las Vegas (~130 artists), Ultra, HARD Summer, Bassrush Massive and Circus Reloaded generate spurious overlap — any two artists in bass music co-occur on a festival compilation. Only curated multi-artist releases under ~15 artists counted.
