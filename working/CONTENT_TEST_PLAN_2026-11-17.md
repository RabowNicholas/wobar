# Content test plan (2026-11-17)

Built from working/artist_content_finder_2026-11-13/REPORT_CONTENT.md (artist review) and working/format_trends_finder_2026-11-17/TEMPLATES.md (format accounts). Decisions below are Nick's, made in session.

## Main page: 4 formats, all split screen with Nick reacting (quiet, not performed)

| # | Format | Setting | Second hook | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **samples i'll never get cleared** | Studio: Nick + DAW (visualizer once one exists) | Confession + CTA, word for word every time: "comment ID and i'll send you this song" | "samples i'll never get cleared #0X: [song]" (name the sample in plain words) |
| 2 | **POV: [mundane task]** | Everyday footage (dinner, groceries, gym), deadpan | The drop lands on an action (knife hits board, cart hits shelf) | One specific detail ("the cereal aisle", not "grocery shopping") |
| 3 | **flip, tested live** | Show footage, crowd | Crowd reaction | Optional: "played the [song] flip at [venue]" |
| 4 | **testing this one in [place]** | Liminal spaces: parking garage, tunnel, empty pool, highway at 3am | The place changes the sound | "testing the new one in [place] at [time]" |

**Killed:** "what if this song had my sound". In the artist data, 7 of 9 plain "[song] but make it [style]" posts sat at or below the artist's median. Its flips move into format 1 (studio) or 3 (live).

### Datamosh test (inside format 2)
Rotate three versions on the drop:
1. Plain sync, no effect (control)
2. Wireframe overlay intruding on the drop
3. Datamosh: MoshUp motion transfer (render as the motion source, mundane clip as the image). Desaturated, drop only, 1 to 3 seconds, snap back. Backup app: Datamosher Pro (Classic I-frame mode for a void-swallows-the-scene transition).

No research evidence on effects; this is a brand-logic test. Restraint rule applies: an effect used throughout reads as a filter.

### Constant on every post
- One lowercase voice line, never explaining the center
- One grade
- No flyer-style promo; release info in the caption only
- 3 posts a week on fixed days, about 24 posts over 8 weeks
- Reaction kept quiet (a face is permitted, a personality being sold is not)

### Measurement and decisions
- Per post: views, shares, saves, profile visits, link clicks, and whether it reached 2x baseline median.
- Per format: median vs baseline, and share of posts at 2x.
- After 4 posts of a format: under 0.75x baseline, change one thing (hook line or first second).
- After 8 posts: drop the weakest format; give its slot to the strongest or a new candidate.
- Never judge on one post.

## Burner accounts (TikTok, 5 posts a day, Nick's own music only)
- **Account A, the void:** Nick's renders (liminal spaces, PS1 low-poly, megastructures). Found footage (deep sea, space, tunnels) runs as a variable inside A; spin off only if it clearly beats renders.
- **Account B, quiet anime frames:** Lain, Evangelion, Berserk, Blame!. Atmospheric shots only, not fights. Separate account: different community, and it isolates the rights risk.
- **Every post:** b-roll + one text line + one section of the song. Never reuse a clip (repetition filters).
- **Phase 1 variables:** imagery (per account), audio treatment (original, slowed, sped up, edit cut), song section (drop, build, hook/quiet).
- **Phase 2:** text type, first second. **Phase 3:** loop, length, grade, series framing.
- **Score:** views only. Share of posts over 10k across the first 30 posts per account. No cross-boosting between accounts.
