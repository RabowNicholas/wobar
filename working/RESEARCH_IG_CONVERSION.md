# DOES INSTAGRAM CONVERT?

> **⚠ SUPERSEDED IN PART — read [[working/RESEARCH_VERIFICATION_MEMO]] first.**
> - **Study A's conclusion is downgraded to UNSUPPORTED.** The IG↔Spotify relationship is **untested**, not disproven. Partial correlation controlling for SoundCloud followers drops r from 0.938 to 0.591 (not significant); IG and SC followers correlate at r=0.974, so Instagram adds no independent signal beyond any other size proxy. The one peer-reviewed source measures Facebook/Twitter and PRS royalties on a 2016–18 UK roster — not this. **Do not use the sentence "there's no streaming problem, there's a follower problem"** — that's an interventional claim from a cross-sectional correlation shown to be a scale artifact. The artist "spüke" has been **removed** from the analysis at Nick's instruction.
> - **Study B SURVIVES and strengthens.** Audience is not a label gate: Jadū Dala released Vulora at ≤2,324 IG followers / 119 monthly listeners.
> - **[UNSUPPORTED] The "labels produce listeners" inference in §A3/§B2.** No difference-in-differences, synthetic control, matched-cohort or event study on label signing exists anywhere. Proceed on cost, not proven return.
> - **§1's funnel observation stands as fact and remains unexplained as a diagnosis** — 9,148 reached → 706 profile visits → 20 link taps is real and objectively poor; nothing in this research identifies which intervention would move it.


Every prior file in this session measured the **top** of the funnel. This one measures whether the top connects to anything.

## A1 — The correlation is weak, and one pair kills it outright

**spüke: 5,453 IG followers → 98,468 monthly listeners.**
**ISEEU: 5,638 IG followers → 2,831 monthly listeners.**

Near-identical Instagram followings. **35× difference in listeners.**

More of the same:
- spüke (5,453 IG) out-listens **Flozone (24,500 IG)** by ~2× and **OkayJake (18,800 IG)** by ~4×, on a fifth of the audience.
- COIDO (4,360 IG) beats OkayJake's ratio 2× over, at a quarter the following.

Instagram size does not predict listener count in this lane. There is a floor effect at the very bottom — Wobar and Vulora are small on both — but that reflects catalogue age and career stage, not IG causing streams.

## A2 — The number that reframes everything

**Wobar: 26 monthly listeners.**

400 IG followers, 116,070 IG views in 90 days, **26 monthly listeners.**

The Placement Plan's machinery — editorial pitching, Source-of-Streams diagnostics, save-rate targets, DIY Meta ads judged on save/completion — is calibrated for an artist with an audience. At 26 monthly listeners there is essentially nothing on the DSP side to convert *to*. IG is not underperforming into a working funnel; the funnel's far end is empty.

## A3 — What actually produces listeners
Not visible in this data, but the spüke/ISEEU pair rules out IG as the driver. The realistic candidates — playlisting, label release infrastructure, DJ support, catalogue depth — are all **Placement Plan levers, not Instagram levers.**

# STUDY B — Does IG or streaming size gate the label doors?

**Method note:** historical follower counts aren't observable. But **current count is an upper bound on count-at-signing** — an artist who has 2,324 followers today had ≤2,324 when signed. That makes small current numbers a valid one-directional proof.

| Artist | Label + release | IG followers (upper bound at signing) | Monthly listeners |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vulora** | **Jadū Dala** — *Want Me 2* | **2,324** (16 posts) | **119** |
| **Vanda** | **Jadū Dala** — *cantbelievemyeyes* | **1,724** | not captured |
| **ISEEU** | **Jadū Dala** — *CHECKS UP*; Bassrush comp | **5,638** (22 posts) | **2,831** |
| COIDO | Wormhole Music Group | 4,360 | 11,558 |
| Crucial | Wubaholics — *VESPER*, *NIGHT SHIFT* | 4,767 | 15,737 |

## B1 — Neither metric is a gate

**Jadū Dala released Vulora at ≤2,324 Instagram followers and 119 monthly listeners.** That is a lower audience than Wobar has on Instagram today, and roughly 5× Wobar's listener count — from a label that is currently open for submissions and sits at the centre of Wobar's actual lane.

Same label released Vanda at ≤1,724 followers.

**The doors Wobar wants are not audience-gated. They are taste-gated.** This confirms and sharpens the Placement Plan's already-verified finding that "boutique labels sign at 5–20k monthly listeners" is a myth — the real threshold is lower than the myth *and* lower than Wobar's current position on at least one axis.

## B2 — Consequence
The anxiety about 400 followers is misdirected with respect to the label lever. **Wobar could submit to Jadū Dala, Wubaholics, Local Void, Gravitas or Deep Dark & Dangerous today and be within the audience range those labels demonstrably sign at.** What gates it is the music and the fit, which is exactly what the Placement Plan's reserve-track rule exists to serve.

# WHAT THIS DOES TO THE STRATEGY

1. **IG is not the listener engine.** Study A rules it out — same IG size, 35× listener spread. Judging Instagram on streams was always going to fail; now there's evidence rather than assumption.
2. **IG is not the label gate.** Study B rules it out — Jadū Dala signs at 119 monthly listeners.
3. **Therefore IG's remaining defensible jobs are narrow:** credibility surface for promoters/A&R who look before they book, and feeder into the Ether (where the text-to-number CTA already demonstrably converts in this audience, and link-in-bio demonstrably does not — 20 taps / 90 days).
4. **The real bottleneck is the catalogue and its distribution, not the content strategy.** 26 monthly listeners against 116K IG views in 90 days is not a social-media problem.
5. **The Placement Plan is the higher-leverage document, and it always was.** This session's research keeps arriving back at it: Local Void open, Gravitas open, DDD open by email, SubCarbon open, Deadbeats reinstated, Denver corridor unchanged — and none of them require an Instagram audience.

## Open / not answered
- What *does* drive monthly listeners in this lane (playlisting vs label vs DJ support vs catalogue depth). The spüke/ISEEU pair proves it isn't IG but doesn't say what it is. That is the next genuinely useful study.
- Wobar's own Spotify catalogue state — release count, playlist placements, Source of Streams — not audited this session.
- Whether IG matters for **bookings** specifically. Untested; promoters were assumed to check IG, never verified.
