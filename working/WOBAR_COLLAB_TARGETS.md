# COLLAB TARGETS — THE LADDER

> **Provenance.** Every artist name here comes from the vault — Nick's own 93-minute benchmark set, the six-register table, or the browser-verified Beatport sweeps of 2026-07-27. **The tier placement is mine and nothing external validates it.** No follower counts appear in this file by design: the vault has been burned twice by borrowed numbers read as first-party results. Tiers are qualitative bands, and a name in the wrong band costs an approach, not a strategy.

---

## §0 — FIVE THINGS THAT SHAPE THE LIST

**1. It is structured by register inside each tier, never flat.** A flat collab list is the exact failure that put psy-bass comps in front of wonky-140 labels ([[WOBAR_CONTEXT]] Drift Rule §3). Wobar's music spans six registers at a target weighting; a collaborator is a collaborator *for a register*, not for the project.

**2. Collabs are not won by submission at any tier.** The vault settled this on 2026-07-27: **T1 entry is submission-shaped, T3/T4 entry is collaboration-shaped, and the rung between them isn't climbed — it's introduced.** Nothing above T3 opens by asking. It opens because someone at T3 says a name.

**3. Flow State is the only currency in this project, and it is calibrated to exactly one rung.** A booking is enormous to an artist at 3K and worthless to one at 50K ([[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §69). **T3 is not the consolation rung — it is the only rung where Nick has something to trade today**, and he is on the buying side, which almost nobody at this stage is.

**4. The second currency is the visualizer, and it is an offer rather than an ask.** Make one for a rung-up artist's track and send it free — competence proof, rolodex and borrowed reach in one object. Works T2–T3. It is the best idea the socials session produced and it has never been used.

**5. The realistic first "collab" is a tracklist, not a joint track.** A 29-artist VA puts Wobar's name beside twenty-eight others for one track and no relationship ([[working/RESEARCH_LABEL_LANDSCAPE]] §2.6). Cheaper than any collaboration on this page and it produces the introduction that §0.2 says is the only way up.

A collab in register 4 is a SoundCloud object by construction — marketing, never a listener source. That is a constraint on the format, not a reason to skip the row: it is 16% of runtime and the artists there are SoundCloud-native by necessity, which also means no Beatport-shaped method will ever find them.

## §3 — WHAT THE ASK ACTUALLY IS, BY RUNG

| Tier | What is realistically available | What Wobar trades |
|---|---|---|
| **T0** | Nothing | — |
| **T1** | An official remix slot · a flip they play · a support booking | Nothing yet. Requires the music to clear the bar first — still the binding constraint |
| **T2** | A remix · a VA tracklist beside them · a play in their set | The **free visualizer**. Competence proof that costs them nothing to accept |
| **T3** ⭐ | **A genuine collab** · a co-release · a repost · a play | **A Flow State booking** — the one non-replicable asset, and it is worth the most exactly here |
| **T4** | A collab this month | Reciprocity. Nothing else needed |
| **T5** | A collab, a B2B, a bill | Nick is already the buyer |

**The sequencing that falls out of it:** the list is worked from the bottom. T4/T5 produce the relationships, T3 produces the introduction, and T2/T1 are entered on someone else's word. Nothing on this page is entered by asking the person at the top.

## §4 — OPEN

- **T4 and T5 are empty and only Nick can fill them.** Until they are, the ladder has no bottom, which is the half that actually moves.
- **Tier placement is unverified judgment.** No follower data was used deliberately; the cost is that a name may sit one band off. Cheap to correct, worth correcting before an approach.
- **Whether a collab is even the right instrument at T2/T1.** A remix slot and a support booking are both easier and possibly worth more, and neither is a collaboration. This file currently treats them as adjacent; they may deserve their own ladder.
- **Register 4 has no distributable format.** Every rap-flip collab is a SoundCloud object. Unsolved, and it applies to 16% of the target architecture.
- **The binding constraint is unchanged and sits above all of this:** whether the music clears the bar. Zero of these conversations survive a track that doesn't.
