## §8 — WHAT THE ASK IS, BY RUNG

| Rung | Available | What Wobar trades |
|---|---|---|
| **§4 ceiling** | A remix slot · a flip they play · a support booking. **Not a collab** | Nothing yet |
| **§5 chart-evidenced** | A VA tracklist beside them · a play | The **free visualizer** — competence proof that costs them nothing to accept |
| **§3 measured band** ⭐ | **A genuine collab · a co-release · a repost** | **A Flow State booking** — *"enormous to an artist at 3K and worthless to one at 50K,"* and this band is 1.7K–24K |
| **§7 roster population** | A collab this month | Reciprocity |
| **Local / SLC** | A collab · a B2B · a bill | Nick is already the buyer |

**Two rails that constrain every row:**

- **Repost outreach and label doors conflict.** A repost publishes; labels require unreleased **and unpublished**. Collab tracks intended for a door stay on private links.
- **Register 4 cannot reach DSPs.** Uncleared flips are permanently non-distributable and non-monetisable — **FLY, SAKA and Tape B collabs are SoundCloud objects by construction**, and no Beatport-shaped method will ever find that half of the lane. 16% of the target architecture with no distributable format.

## §9 — OWED AND OPEN

- **🔴 Local / SLC is empty and only Nick can fill it.** The vault holds zero local artist names; every discovery method run is structurally incapable of surfacing them.
- **🔴 The §7 population is unranked.** 56 handles resolved, view sweep never run. Running it converts ~55 names from a population into a ranked tier — the highest-value unrun task pointed at this file.
- **Nobody has been contacted.** 0 demos sent, and no collab approach has ever been made.
- **Four dream names run a submittable door and none has been used** — Zeds Dead (Deadbeats, open), INZO (Local Void, open), LSDREAM (Trippy Bee, open), Of The Trees (Memory Palace, closed).
- **Whether a collab is the right instrument above the measured band.** A remix slot and a support booking are easier and possibly worth more, and neither is a collaboration. They may deserve their own ladder.
- **The binding constraint sits above all of it:** whether the music clears the bar.
