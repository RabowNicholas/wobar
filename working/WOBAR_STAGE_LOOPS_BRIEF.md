# WOBAR — Stage Visuals, Phase 1

## What Wobar is

Wobar is my music project: dark, wonky 140 bass. Everything I make lives in one visual world: low-poly wireframe forms sitting inside real spaces. On the releases you see that world from the outside. It tells a story and invites you in. At the show it's different: you're not looking at the world anymore, you're inside it. I want people to feel lost, hypnotized, like reality is tearing. That's the part I need help building.

## The feeling

Psychedelic as a feeling, not as color. Your perception stops being reliable: depth flips, you can't tell how big something is, patterns almost resolve and then don't, and things move without you catching them move.

Lost means pulled down and in, never floating up and away. No space, no ascending. You're on a dancefloor with the sub in your chest, so there's a floor under all of this.

## What holds the world together

- **Line, not surface.** Wireframe is the material of the world.
- **Field, not object.** At the show you're inside the structure, so it fills the frame. No single hero object. This is also practical: a field survives any crop.
- **Muted color.** The disorientation should come from line and contrast, not saturation. Two starting palettes:
  - Purple: `#190028 → #2D0546 → #5A0F78 → #6B2E87 → #A085AE`
  - Green: `#0E0813 → #1E505A → #4D6149 → #5C7470 → #7A8A6F`

  No pure white, no neon.
- **What breaks it:**
  - Figures, faces, or recognizable objects. People should project onto it, not be told what they're looking at.
  - Mandalas, kaleidoscopes, sacred geometry.
  - Space and nebulas.
  - Synthwave grids.
  - Text.

## Phase 1: four loops

One per energy level: **Low · Building · Peak · Settling.**

How each one moves and what it shows is your call. Peak is the one place the world is allowed to break its own structure.

## Start here

Before building anything, send me your read: how you'd approach this, and one frame or rough sketch for any of the four. If it clicks, we go from there.

## Specs

- **Tempo:** 140 BPM, bar-locked. 16-bar loops (64 beats, 27.43s), seamless. Set clip length in beats so the VJ software handles frame rounding. Must also sit at 70 BPM halftime.
- **Composition:** has to work cropped to 16:9, 9:16, square, and odd LED shapes. Nothing essential in the center or at the edges.
- **Spotify Canvas:** a seamless 4-bar loop (6.86s) per energy level, 9:16, no audio.
- **Delivery, per loop:**
  - 3840×3840 ProRes 422 HQ master
  - 1920×1080 HAP
  - 9:16 Canvas cut
  - Graded and grayscale versions of each
  - Project files
- **Built procedurally, ideally in TouchDesigner.** Expose speed, density, and displacement as parameters. Phase 2 turns this into a real-time, audio-reactive system driven by the kick and the sub.

If this world clicks for you and you want that, the door is open.
