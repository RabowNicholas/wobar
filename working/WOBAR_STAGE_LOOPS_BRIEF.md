# Wobar — stage visuals

## What Wobar is

Wobar is my music project. Dark, wonky 140 bass. Everything I put out lives in one visual world: low-poly wireframe forms sitting inside real spaces. On the releases you're looking at that world from the outside. It tells a story and pulls you in. I'm sending the release packet for my EP Hohlweg along with this so you can see what that looks like. It's just one example of it.

At the show I want that to flip. You're not looking at the world anymore, you're inside of it. The words I keep coming back to are lost, hypnotized, like reality is tearing. That's the part I need help building.

## The feeling

When I say psychedelic I mean the feeling, not the colors. I want people to stop trusting what they're seeing. You can't tell if something is close or far, big or small. You look back and it's changed, and you didn't see it happen.

The one thing I don't want is the floaty cosmic thing. No space, no ascending into the light. It's more like getting pulled under. People are on a dancefloor with the sub in their chest, so it can get pretty disorienting and still feel like it has weight.

## The world

A few things that make it feel like Wobar to me:

- It's all lines. Wireframe is what the world is made of.
- At the show you're inside the structure, so it should fill the whole frame instead of being one object in the middle. That also means it holds up however it gets cropped, which matters (more on that in the specs).
- Color stays muted. I'd rather the disorientation come from the lines and the contrast than from saturation. Four palettes to start from. Each one runs dark to light, shadows on the left, brightest highlight on the right:
  - Purple: `#190028` `#2D0546` `#5A0F78` `#6B2E87` `#A085AE`
  - Green: `#0E0813` `#1E505A` `#4D6149` `#5C7470` `#7A8A6F`
  - Blue: `#0E0813` `#2C4554` `#3B4C73` `#4A6479` `#948C9E`
  - Rust: `#0E0813` `#6F4E3A` `#8A5A3D` `#A6633D` `#C7BBA6`

  Even the brightest color is never pure white, and nothing's neon.
- Stuff that pulls it out of the world for me: people, faces, recognizable objects, mandala or kaleidoscope stuff, space and nebulas, synthwave grids, text.

## References

I attached three: the cube field with the sphere, the purple contour pattern, and the line terrain. What I love in all of them is the movement. Hypnotic, kind of hard to look at in a good way, your eye never really lands anywhere. They're all way more saturated than what I'm going for though.

## What I'm looking for first

Four loops, one for each energy level: low, building, peak, settling. What they look like and how they move is up to you. Peak is the one where the world can start breaking apart.

Before you build anything, I'd love to just hear how you'd approach it. A quick explanation is perfect. If it clicks, we go from there.

## Specs

- 140 BPM, locked to the bar. Each loop is 16 bars (64 beats, about 27.4 seconds) and loops clean.
- 30 fps.
- These are going to end up on all kinds of screens: wide, vertical, square, weird LED shapes. I'll cut the vertical versions out of the middle of the wide frame myself, so it has to hold up if I crop a vertical slice out of the center.
- For each loop:
  - 3840×2160 MP4, 10-bit, high bitrate
  - graded and grayscale versions
  - project files
- If you can set it up so things like speed, density and displacement are easy to dial in later, that'd be huge. Eventually I want this reacting to the music live.

## Where this goes

This starts as a paid job for four loops, but honestly I'm not just looking for loops. I'm looking for someone to build the live side of Wobar with me. Down the line that's a fully audio-reactive show and someone on the team running it. If this world clicks for you and that sounds like something you'd want, the door's open.

We'll sort out budget and timing when we talk, and I want all of that settled before you start anything. I want to make sure you're well paid for this.
