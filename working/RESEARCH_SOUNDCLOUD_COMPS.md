## §1 — In this lane SoundCloud is the bigger audience, and Wobar has it inverted

Of the artists with both platforms measured, **almost all have SoundCloud ≥ Instagram** — often substantially: Acyan 2.4×, ISEEU 2.0×, dêtre 1.6×, Gunpoint 1.3×. Only Crucial and COIDO are Instagram-heavier.

**Wobar is the opposite: 400 on Instagram, 55 on SoundCloud.**

## §2 — The real gap is here, not on Instagram

On Instagram, Wobar sits 4–60× behind the peer set. Normalised per-follower, it's mid-band (see RESEARCH_IG_COMPS §2).

**On SoundCloud, Wobar is 34× behind the smallest account in the sample and ~940× behind the largest.** Crucial at 1,865 is the floor of the peer band; 55 is not on the chart.

Six months of Instagram optimisation went into the platform where the deficit was smallest, on the metric that turned out not to matter.

## §5 — The convergence this produces

Nick's stated differentiator is **the journey — the arc across a set**. It only survives at set length; it is invisible in a single track.

- The format where his differentiator survives is the **mix/set**.
- The mix/set is the **best-performing format on SoundCloud**.
- SoundCloud is the **platform his lane actually lives on**.
- And a numbered series is a format **almost nobody in his Instagram comp set is running**.

He also appears to have already started one — the SoundCloud player showed **"Wobar — Portal Journey 002"** throughout this sweep.

That is the first time in this entire session that the brand position, the differentiator, the platform, and the measured best-performing format all point at the same object.

## §6 — Two mechanics worth noting
- **Reposts carry reach.** COIDO's two most recent profile items are a **Wormhole Music Group** repost (3,654 plays) and a **Rinse FM** repost (1,788). His reach is being carried by a label and a radio show. Reposts arrive with labels — another reason the label lever feeds everything else.
- **Phone numbers in bios again.** Acyan lists "say hi: +1 (970) 717-4013" on SoundCloud, as he does on Instagram. Consistent with Good Society, Flow State, and the Mirror Threshold thesis — direct-contact CTAs are normal in this scene.

## §7 — Wobar's own SoundCloud, cold read
**55 followers · 72 following · 11 tracks · Artist Pro.** Latest track *Kekkai* (2 days ago) — **196 plays, 3 likes**. World-A-Reggae flip 16 days ago.

The bio is the world doc surfaced almost verbatim — five dense paragraphs opening "wobar builds bass music structured for inward confrontation… the sound functions as mirror." Against the five-second read an A&R or DJ gives a profile, that is a wall. The world is present but not legible at a glance.

## §8 — What this changes
1. **SoundCloud is the priority surface**, not Instagram. The audience, the A&R listening, the DJ sends and the repost mechanic all live there.
2. **Flips go to SoundCloud** and should be framed as flips of recognisable records — that's the observed engine.
3. **The mix/set series is the highest-value unexploited format**, and it's the one that carries the arc. *Portal Journey* already exists as a numbered series.
4. **The bio needs cutting** to something readable in five seconds, with the world showing rather than explaining.
5. The Instagram wipe-and-rebuild is fine, but it's now the *secondary* surface.
