# Listening notes, 2026-11-11

From Nick listening to the "Label study" Spotify playlists (last 12 months of each label's releases).

## Clear matches

- **Trippy Bee Records**: possibly the best fit on the list. The sound design is already naturally here. Very psychedelic, heavy hip hop influence in nearly every track. "Weird music" is the best word.
- **Aspire Higher**: dark experimental 140. Fits Act 3/4.
- **Odyzey Music**: world bass. Fits wobar.lite and Acts 1, 2 and 5.
- **1985 Music**: dark minimal 140 and DnB. Great fit for certain songs, not fully Wobar's sound.
- **DUPLOC**: more strictly 140. Less of an immediate fit, still possible.
