## Rules as finally used (what calibration changed)

- Genre: 8 of last 12 releases in 140 + Dubstep together (release genre = majority of its tracks). Odyzey and Trippy Bee count as fully in-genre by your override.
- Activity: **2** releases in 6 months (was 3) and 1 since 2026-09-12. Changed for Locus Sound.
- Roster: 5+ artists in 2 years, 3+ not the owner.
- No-chart labels: roster strength (RSI) **40** (was 60), with peer-label artists adding 8 (3 to 4) or 14 (5+). Changed for Locus Sound.
- Identity: **SoundCloud confirmed by 3+ matching titles.** The owner-statement requirement was dropped after it sent DUPLOC and Trippy Bee to manual review. Unstated owners are reported, not used to drop a label.
- Hype: no Hype chart exists for either genre, so Hype points went to chart proof (40) and roster (30).
- Followers count only from confirmed accounts. Distributors (Create Music Group, DistroKid, UnitedMasters) are not labels.

## Limits

- One chart snapshot. Chart proof is provisional; re-pull in two weeks and save weekly snapshots to firm it up.
- No address was tested. Doors come only from label pages and bios, copied exactly, read today, except Memory Palace's "not accepting demos" (from July, not re-verified) and Wubaholics' form rules (from August).
- LabelRadar, Hypeddit and Monstercat/Gravitas demo pages were not opened past their links; check for credits or fees before using them.
- Artist footprints only count labels releasing in 140 and Dubstep, so artists who mostly release elsewhere look narrower than they are.
- Label catalogues are the latest 100 tracks per id.
- SoundCloud identity is by titles. Deadbeats matched 0 titles; it's accepted because its own bio links its demo form.
