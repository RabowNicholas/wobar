# LABEL MAP

## §0 — LANE RESOLVED (2026-07-26, from Nick's latest set)

The two-lane frame below is **incomplete**. Nick's most recent 93-minute set — his own best example of the journey — shows the center of mass sits in a **third lane** neither Lane A nor Lane B covers.

### The arc, by runtime

| Segment | Time | Share | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Deep / hypnotic** | 0:00–19:00 | 20% | Super Future, Comisar/Daggz/Chmura, **Mur (Wobar)**, Yoko, Potions, En:vy, Jungle Rootz |
| **2. Deep-dubstep hinge** | 19:00–22:12 | 3% | Distinct Motive — *No Mercy* |
| **3. Dark / wonky 140** | 22:12–49:15 | **29%** | OkayJake+STVSH, **Crease (Wobar)**, Into Stone, Daggz, Gunpoint+highrollr, dêtre, Blurrd Vzn, STVSH, Milano, Quackson, Crucial+STVSH |
| **4. Rap-flip peak** | 49:15–1:04:23 | **16%** | FLY, Ero808+Coka Cobra, **Down Bad (Wobar flip)**, FLY×SAKA, SPOONE flip, Noctiil flip, Tape B×STVSH flip |
| **5. Dub pivot** | 1:04:23–1:11:14 | 7% | Distinct Motive+Caspa, **World-A-Reggae (Wobar flip)** |
| **6. Psy-bass emotional outro** | 1:11:14–1:33:06 | 24% | Alex Unger, Of The Trees ×2, INZO ×3, LSDREAM, **Even In Darkness (Wobar)** |

### What it shows

1. **The vault's comps describe the outro, not the identity.** `WOBAR_SONIC.md` names Of The Trees / Shlump / INZO. Of The Trees and INZO appear **only in the final 22 minutes**. Shlump does not appear at all. The psy-bass register is Wobar's *resolution*, not his center.
2. **The most-played artist in the set is STVSH — 4 appearances** (Left Behind, CROOKED, VESPER, Tape B×STVSH flip). Not Of The Trees, not INZO, not Distinct Motive.
3. **Center of mass = segments 3 + 4 = 45% of runtime**: US dark/wonky 140 + rap-flip culture. STVSH, Blurrd Vzn, Tape B, SAKA, FLY, Gunpoint, Crucial, dêtre, Ero808, Coka Cobra, Quackson, Milano.
4. **Wobar's own originals are the structural pillars of the arc** — *Mur* opens the deep section, *Crease* anchors the dark middle, *Even In Darkness* carries the emotional outro, plus two flips at the peak/pivot. He writes across the full arc rather than in one register. This is the strongest available evidence that the arc, not the sound, is the identity.
5. **The deep-dubstep content is real but small** — Distinct Motive ×2 (one with Caspa), ~10% of runtime. Lane B is a genuine influence and a legitimate track-level door, but it is not where the set lives.

Ecosystem: SoundCloud-first, self-release and small imprints, **UKF** and **Deadbeats** as the visible rungs, festival/club circuit US-wide. **This lane is by far the most Instagram-native of the three — flip culture runs on short-form.**

### The two findings that follow

- **Tape B × Of The Trees — *Brackish*, 2026.** The two ends of Wobar's set are already collaborating. The arc is not eccentric; it is the direction the scene is moving. That is a positioning asset, not a positioning problem.
- **Deadbeats now appears three separate times** in this research — Distinct Motive (*Dubs EP*, 2025), STVSH (*FRICTION*, 2026), and as Zeds Dead's imprint. Its **DROPPED** verdict in [[working/WOBAR_PLACEMENT_PLAN]] was made on psy-bass grounds and does not survive this evidence. **Re-open it.**

### Corrections owed to the vault
- `reference/WOBAR_SONIC.md` comps are outro-only and need a rewrite to reflect the actual center of mass.
- Placement Plan lever weighting assumes the psy-bass ladder. Lane C has different doors and a different discovery mechanic.
- Flips are treated in CONTENT.md as SoundCloud feeder material. In Lane C, **flips are the primary discovery vector** — and Wobar's own IG data agrees (World-A-Reggae flip = his best-performing owned non-event post).

## The headline — the two named artists are in different lanes

Nick named **EAZYBAKED** and **Distinct Motive** as reference points. They do not share a label ecosystem. This matters more than any individual label on the list.

| | EAZYBAKED | Distinct Motive |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Denver, CO | Toronto, CA |
| Sound | Psy/experimental bass, festival-scale | Deep, minimal 140 dubstep |
| Labels | **Wakaan · Deadbeats · HypnoVizion · Dome of Doom · Bassrush Records · Memory Palace** | **Deep Dark & Dangerous · Navy Cut · Deadbeats** |
| Ecosystem | US festival circuit, Denver corridor, Spotify/short-form driven | UK/NZ/EU, Bandcamp + vinyl + Beatport, dubplate and DJ-support driven |
| Notes | 2022 album *INTERTWINED* incl. "Sapped" w/ Of The Trees; *RESPAWN* EP 2025 self-released | 5 vinyl releases 2016–19; *Dubs EP* on Deadbeats, Mar 2025; support from Mala, Truth, J:Kenzo, J.Sparrow |

**Deadbeats is the only overlap** — and the Placement Plan currently has Deadbeats marked DROPPED as "too broad/mainstream." That call was made against the psy-bass lane. It does not hold for the deep-dubstep lane, where Deadbeats is where Distinct Motive landed. Worth revisiting depending on the lane answer below.

**The open question this forces:** Wobar's IG bio says **"deep dubstep."** `reference/WOBAR_SONIC.md` names the comps as **Of The Trees / Shlump / INZO** — all psy-bass. Those are two different careers with two different label ladders, two different geographies, and — critically — **two different roles for Instagram.** This needs resolving before the label lever or the content strategy can be aimed.

## The bridge — DMVU

**DMVU is the single most useful artist on this map for Wobar.** Denver-based. Came up through **Encrypted Audio** (UK deep dubstep), released the *Bruk EP* on **Deep Dark & Dangerous** (NZ), and is on the **Memory Palace** roster (Denver psy-bass). He is the proof that the two lanes are traversable, and the template for doing it from the American side.

If the lane question resolves as "deep dubstep, but Denver-connected," DMVU's path is the map.

## What this changes for the Placement Plan

1. **CLOSED — Local Void portal verified open** (2026-07-26, `localvoid.com/submissions`). This was an open item; it remains the START-HERE target for Lane A.
2. **Gravitas confirmed open** with a hard requirement: finished + mastered + unreleased. Reserve-track rule is load-bearing, exactly as the plan assumed.
3. **Deadbeats "DROPPED" needs re-examination.** It was cut as too broad for psy-bass, but it is where Distinct Motive released in 2025. Lane-dependent call.
4. **An entire second ladder exists that the Placement Plan doesn't contain** — the Lane B deep dubstep labels. These have real open doors (DDD takes demos by email), are small enough to answer, and reward exactly the thing Wobar is strongest at (sound design over festival spectacle). If the bio is honest, this is the ladder.
5. **Lane changes the IG answer too.** Lane A is US-festival, Denver-corridor, short-form-driven — Instagram matters a lot. Lane B runs on SoundCloud, Bandcamp, vinyl, Beatport, dubplates and DJ support — Instagram matters considerably less. **Do not finalize the content strategy before the lane question is answered.**
