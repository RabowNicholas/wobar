# OUTSIDE-VIEW CHECKLIST — COVERS + FLYERS

**The progression this sits in:** cover = the tear held small · flyer = the tear opening · show = fully inside. Covers and flyers are the outside view; the stage is the inside.

A cover that fails any item isn't done.

## Form
- [ ] One focal form, and it's abstract: no creature, craft, or recognizable object.
- [ ] Real wireframe geometry, lit by the scene, casting its own fractured shadow.
- [ ] It stands **in** the space, not pasted on top. An overlay is a fail.
- [ ] No old-video-game nostalgia read.

## World
- [ ] The environment is plain, intact ground, ordinary enough that the wrongness lands.
- [ ] The viewer is standing somewhere: gravity, ground, a horizon. Being here, not leaving.

## Color
- [ ] Graded with the Green or Purple preset: Soft Light at 40–70%, black point lifted, white point capped.
- [ ] No pure white, no neon, including in the photoreal half.

## Restraint
- [ ] Nothing explains the meaning. Nothing announces the theme.
- [ ] The CA/bloom/grain stack is finishing, not the substance.
- [ ] It still reads at Spotify thumbnail size.
- [ ] It couldn't belong to any other bass artist.

## Type
- [ ] The diamond-O wordmark is the real asset, not retyped.
- [ ] Geometric bold, all caps, tracking +50 to +100, max two fonts.

## Flyers only
- [ ] It's the invitation to the breach. Still the outside view, but the tear is open **wider** than on a cover, and the stage field material shows through it without replacing the world.
- [ ] Date and venue are legible without getting in the way of the form.
