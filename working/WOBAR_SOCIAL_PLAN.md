## §0 — THE PRIORITY RULE

**Music first, always. Instagram runs on genuine surplus and never competes with a release.**

Confirmed by Nick 2026-08-12 when the rest of this plan was revised upward — **the expansion of §1 did not buy a claim on the calendar.** The v1.0 line stands unchanged: *if this delays [[working/WOBAR_ROADMAP]] Phase 1 by a single day it has already cost more than it can return.*

**This is what makes the growth goal safe.** §1 says grow it as much as possible; §0 sets what "possible" means. The two are not in tension — they are the same decision, and reading §1 without §0 reproduces the exact failure §7 names.

**Consequence, and it shapes everything downstream:** *post more* is not an available recommendation. See §5 — yield, not volume.

## §1 — THE DECISION

**Instagram is a surface in its own right and growing it is a goal. The audience is lane-weighted — producers, DJs and the people who listen to this music — and the account must keep reading as a producer's account. It does two jobs: it carries the credibility that makes a peer take the DM seriously, and it reads reaction to the music faster than any other surface can.**

**Revised 2026-08-12, superseding the 2026-07-30 lock.** Everything below is downstream of that sentence.

### Why the previous decision fell

v1.0 read: *"peer-facing surface … the output is credibility and conversations. Never scored on followers, reach, or streams."* **It prohibited a goal using evidence that never addressed that goal.**

- spüke-vs-ISEEU shows IG size does not predict **listeners**.
- Vulora shows label doors are not **audience-gated**.

Neither speaks to whether a larger account makes a producer take a DM seriously — which is the actual claim. §7.1 already conceded the peer chain was *"six links, every one unmeasured."* **The prohibition was never better-evidenced than what replaces it. It was only more restrictive.**

**Nick's correction, 2026-08-12:** credibility with peers is a property of a strong account, and 400 followers does not buy the conversation however good the grid is. Content is also not a tax here — he enjoys making it and it is already part of how he shares music, which closes §7.3 differently than that section expected.

✅ **VERIFIED 2026-08-12, first-party, read directly from Insights** *(this paragraph read "pending verification" until the check was run later the same day).* 30 days: **44,756 views · 24,401 viewers · 84.8% non-followers · 466 followers (+66) · 496 profile visits · 27 link taps.**

**Reach *rate* is up only ~16% on the old baseline — what changed is conversion off it:** profile visits 2.1×, link taps 4×, follows from ~0 to +66, and the top organic post went ~314 → 3,831 views. The account was never wiped (128 posts, up from 101). **This is the first evidence the account distributes on its own**, which is the single most consequential update this file was waiting on — it retires the "no format has ever produced a measurable result" line below.

### The second job — reading the music

**Instagram answers faster than SoundCloud.** A snippet reads in days; SoundCloud needs a finished track and moves slowly. **No other surface in this vault does this**, and no prior version assigned the job to anything.

**Scope, decided 2026-08-12 — it never kills a sketch.** The signal lands *after* the track exists and shapes three things:

| It decides | It does not decide |
|---|---|
| **Which finished tracks lead** — release order, what gets pitched | **Which ideas get finished.** The music gets made either way |
| **Sound direction** — read across months, not posts | |
| **Which tracks earn a visual treatment** — where TD effort goes | |

⚠ **The sample is small and the loop must say so.** Organic median is ~205 views, and scoring finished tracks yields a handful of comparisons a quarter rather than many cheap ones. **Sound direction across months is answerable at that size. "Which of these two tracks leads" often is not.** A loop that manufactures a verdict from two posts is producing noise with a decision attached to it.

### The evidence, demoted from prohibition to constraint

*These were the reasons for v1.0's ban. They remain true and none of them contradicts the new decision — they bound it.*

**IG does not produce listeners.** Same IG size, 35× listener spread: spüke 5,453 IG → 98,468 monthly listeners; ISEEU 5,638 IG → 2,831. ([[working/RESEARCH_IG_CONVERSION]] Study A.)

**IG is not the label gate.** Jadū Dala released Vulora at ≤2,324 IG followers, 119 monthly listeners, **16 posts**. Vanda at ≤1,724. The doors are taste-gated, not audience-gated. (Study B — survives verification.)

**⚠ THE INVERSION — the one objection that survives, because it is about peer perception.** Peers run SoundCloud ≥ Instagram: Acyan 2.4×, ISEEU 2.0×, dêtre 1.6×, Gunpoint 1.3×. Wobar is inverted at 400 IG / 55 SC — ⚠ **the 400 is stale as of 2026-08-12 and the inversion has almost certainly deepened.**

This argument does **not** fall with the rest, because it speaks to exactly the thing the new decision claims — how the account reads to a lane producer. **IG-heavy and SC-light reads as a content account to the precise people the credibility is for.**

It is not an argument against growing Instagram. It is a constraint on doing it alone: **a growing IG against a flat SoundCloud makes the credibility problem worse, not better.** The ratio is the thing to watch, not either number.

**Fans are made by duration, not reach — now a scope boundary, not an objection.** A Reel decides in 3 seconds; a track is 4 minutes, a mix 45, a set 90. 116,070 views in 90 days produced ~0 follows. Corroborated inside one account: **Swomp's numbered mix series 36.3K plays vs 1,232 on his most recent single track.** Under v1.0 this was a reason not to invest. Under this decision it simply means **fan-making is not Instagram's job** — credibility and reading the music are. Do not score it on returns, and do not expect it to produce listeners directly.

### ⚠ Correction to the record — the 316-sends finding is void

[[working/RESEARCH_IG_MECHANICS]] §1 concluded from a post at **11,463 views / 316 sends (2.8% sends-per-reach)** that *"distribution is not the constraint; the system did its job, the account failed to convert."*

**That post was a collaboration with Flow State, whose account always does those numbers.** The reach was borrowed. Wobar's account never demonstrated distribution.

Consequences:
- **"Distribution is not the constraint" is UNSUPPORTED.** Distribution may well be a constraint.
- §3.3's "utility travels without converting" is reattributed — those were Flow State's followers seeing a co-branded post, not Wobar's followers declining to follow.
- Organic median was ~205 views (visualizers 133–314), and **removing the collab, no format on this account had ever produced a measurable result** — ✅ **true as of 2026-07-30, and DEAD as of 2026-08-12.** The Insights read above settles it: the top organic post now does 3,831 views and the account converts off its own reach. The account can distribute without borrowing.
- Same error class as the six failed-lookups-treated-as-findings logged 2026-07-27: **a borrowed number read as a first-party result.**

**The reframe that survives:** the only time Wobar content ever reached anyone, another account carried it. That is the same shape as reposts, VA comps and DJ plays — **borrowed audience is the only distribution this project has ever had.**

### The honest position statement

**Wobar has a local presence and a skill set. Wobar does not have an audience.**

22 monthly listeners is not underperformance — it is the arithmetic of 11 tracks, 0 label releases, 0 demos sent, no Beatport presence and no distribution relationships. **Nothing is broken. Nothing is built yet.** This is a cold start, not a conversion problem, and the two require opposite responses.

## §2 — THE TARGET

### The tier: one rung up

| Tier | Who | Responds? | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Peers** (0–2K) | same stage | **yes, high** | Reciprocity. Content lands here — they still read feeds |
| **One rung up** (2K–20K) | past hungry, not yet inundated | **yes, and it matters** | **← the leverage tier** |
| **Established** (20K+) | Phrva, flozone, STVSH | **no** | Not a content audience. They arrive via the tier below |

**Target the rung above.** They still answer DMs, their audience is 10–50× Wobar's so a repost is real borrowed reach, and they remember being at the bottom.

**The decisive argument: Flow State only has value to people at or slightly above this stage.** A booking is enormous to an artist at 3K and worthless to one at 50K. The one non-replicable asset in this project is precisely calibrated to the rung-up tier.

**You climb one rung at a time.** The 2K artist engages at 55 followers. The 50K artist does not. Skipping rungs does not work.

### Where they are — instruments, not sweeps

1. **[[working/IG_COMP_SAMPLE_FRAME]] — ~240 names already built and never used.** Every artist credited on a Jadū Dala (~140) or Wubaholics (~98) release. Assembled as a research sample; it is a targeting list.
2. **VA compilation tracklists.** 20–29 artists per volume, label-vetted, mostly small. **Each volume is a pre-assembled cohort at this tier.** Ten live series ([[working/RESEARCH_LABEL_LANDSCAPE]] §2.6).
3. **SoundCloud reposts, both directions** — who reposts the lane artists, and who the lane artists repost.
4. **Comment sections of lane artists' posts.** Small artists live there.
5. **Flow State support and opener slots.** Regional artists Nick can actually book.

**Known gap:** the comp set has no peer tier. Crucial at 1,865 SC is the floor; everyone else is 10K+. Flagged in [[working/RESEARCH_IG_MECHANICS]] §6 and still open — *"their numbers show what works at scale, not what breaks a cold start."*

### Bucket 1 — PERFORMANCE
Joe's job for it is **Discovery**, and on his list this bucket, not memes, is the top of the funnel.

> **⚠ Revised 2026-08-04 — bucket 1 is unblocked.** v1.1 read *"capture is parked, no footage, no bucket, the funnel has no mouth."* **That was overstated and is now void.** Two corrections, both Nick's:
> 1. **Footage exists.** The full Flow State set is published on YouTube (1:39:11, verified [[working/WOBAR_SURFACES]] §3). The blocker was never *no footage* — it was **no habit**. Those need opposite responses, the same error the plan made once already with cold-start-vs-conversion (§1).
> 2. **The bucket is room-gated, not capture-gated.** Both its sources come from events.

#### The two sources, and the job each actually does

Joe's stated job — *proof you can command a crowd* — is a **promoter's** credential. The target here is producers one rung up (§2), and their question is not whether Nick can hold a room. It is **"would this person play my track"** — a *selection* credential, not a *command* one. **Bucket 1 is structurally better at the credential the target cares about less.** That is not a reason to drop it; it is the reason bucket 1 is not where the weight goes.

| Source | Proves | Value to the peer target | Supply |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Live shows** | command, room, scale | moderate — the promoter credential, not the producer one | exists, ad hoc |
| **Recorded sets** | selection sustained over 90 min | **not a clip surface** — see below | exists (YouTube) |

**Recorded sets are a surface, not a source.** SURFACES already places live at ceiling 3 and the highest duration in the project, and flags YouTube as the vault's most undervalued surface. **The set itself belongs there; only clips cut from it are bucket-1 supply.** Do not let a 90-minute asset get spent as Reel feedstock.

> **⚠ Revised again, same day — bedroom moved out of this bucket to bucket 2 (Nick's call).** Bedroom capture is now **his own songs only**, which makes it music-proof, not performance-proof. Reasoning at [[working/FORMAT_TESTING]] and in bucket 2 below.
>
> **State the cost plainly rather than let a reassignment hide it — the same discipline §3 applied when Joe's list displaced memes.** Bedroom was the only bucket-1 source that needed no booking, no room, and nobody else holding a camera. **Removing it puts bucket 1 entirely back on event cadence.** Supply is now whatever Flow State dates produce. Bucket 1 is unblocked and episodic; it is not a weekly surface and should not be planned as one.
>
> **What is *not* lost: the selection credential.** It moves rather than disappearing — from *what Nick plays* to **what Nick flips**, where it arrives attached to a release instead of a clip. Choosing what to flip is a taste statement, and the flip is an artifact that survives the 3-second surface. **That is a strictly better home for it**, and it is why these two moves fit together rather than trading off.

#### The constraint bucket 2 already learned, applied here
**A dark club shot fails at thumbnail scale for the same reason the visualizers do.** §3.6 governs both. Stage lighting is the one thing in this bucket that reliably produces one legible high-contrast form per frame — a silhouette against a wash reads at thumbnail where a wide crowd shot does not.

#### Shot grammar — RESOLVED
**A face is allowed in frame, always. Facelessness is not the lead.** (Nick, 2026-08-04.) This overturns v1.1's assumption that hands/decks/back-of-head were the ceiling. **It is a brand-level change, not a socials one — the reasoning and its consequences are recorded at the point of decision in [[reference/WOBAR_WORLD]] §4, not here.**

**What that hands to the plan:** the shot-grammar question is closed for every bucket at once, and no edit decision has to be relitigated later. It matters most for bedroom capture — the highest face-exposure format in the plan — which is now in bucket 2.

**What the vault's own data says about the cost — and it is the honest version, not a justification.** [[working/RESEARCH_IG_MECHANICS]] §4 already found: *"Face-vs-faceless does not predict performance in this sample. Consistency of world does."* Shlump is the most face-forward comp and has the **worst** reach efficiency of the four; Of The Trees is largely silhouetted and has the best; elekktronaut is fully faceless at 11–32%. **So showing a face buys nothing measurable here — and costs nothing measurable either.** Read it as *the constraint was free to keep and is free to drop*, not as evidence for the change. The reasoning for the change is cosmological and lives in WORLD §4.

**The rail that comes with it.** Facelessness used to keep the personality vector out for free. It no longer does, and any bucket with a person in frame now carries that job explicitly. **The standing rail in §3.5 is load-bearing rather than precautionary: content must signal competence, not be generally entertaining.** A clip a dark-140 producer recognises is a credential. A clip of a guy enjoying himself is a content account. Same footage, different cut.

### Bucket 2 — MUSIC
Songs over video. Release and flip pushes to SoundCloud. Visualizers. **Bedroom capture, his own songs only.** Joe's job for it is **Ability** — proof you're a serious, valuable producer.

**Its job is not to persuade. Its job is to stop a lane producer dismissing you** when they check the grid after your DM.

**Its binding constraint is legibility, and that constraint was promoted out of this section 2026-08-04** once bucket 1 turned out to have the same failure. See **§3.6**.

#### Bedroom capture — moved here 2026-08-04, and it settles an open §7 question
**Nick's call: bedroom is his own songs only.** It is [[reference/WOBAR_WORLD]] §9 ***Channels*** — bedroom, no ceremony, something came through and got captured — now extended from audio-only to captured-on-video. Do not coin a second name for it. ([[reference/WOBAR_CONTENT]] defines Channels as bedroom *recordings*; this is the same thing with a camera on it, and the release checklist there should absorb rather than duplicate it.)

**Why this is the right bucket, in the plan's own terms.** §7.3 holds an unresolved question: *whether "cheap exhaust" is true — content good enough to earn a lane producer's respect is selected, not exhaust.* **This move answers it for this format.**

| | Is it exhaust? |
|---|---|
| Bedroom **playing other people's tracks** | **No.** Nothing exists at the end but a clip. The session is a production task with no artifact — exactly the "selected, not exhaust" trap §7.3 names |
| Bedroom **playing his own songs** | **Yes.** The track exists whether or not a camera is on. The capture is genuinely a by-product of work already being done |

**That is the test §7 asks to apply to any expansion of this plan** — *does it cause an uncomfortable action, or license a comfortable one?* Bedroom-with-own-songs licenses nothing new; it films the work. Bedroom-as-DJ-selection would have licensed a comfortable new content habit with no release at the end of it.

#### The series — SAMPLES I'LL NEVER GET CLEARED
**Nick's idea, 2026-08-04. Built on the flips.** Format spec, open questions and the kill condition live in **[[working/FORMAT_TESTING]]**; this section records only why it belongs in the plan.

**It is not a new idea against the vault — it is the missing name for a funnel the vault already ranked as THE TRAFFIC.** [[working/WOBAR_ROADMAP]] §C is `flip → numbered series on main SoundCloud → profile visits → back-catalogue of originals`, and [[working/WOBAR_GROWTH_PLAN]] §2 closes its flip section with a literal instruction: ***"Name it, number it, keep it running."*** That instruction has been open since 2026-07-26. This closes it.

**Four things in the vault already argue for it, and none of them were written with this series in mind:**
1. **Flips out-traffic originals ~4×** — Tape B's Mariah flip 867K vs his own original 216K, same account, same playlist, same period. The best-controlled datapoint the vault owns.
2. **The numbered flip series is an established format in this exact lane**, and Tape B's *Vibe Check Vol. 3* is openly uncleared major-label material. **The premise of the title is the scene norm, not a confession.**
3. **Rap-flip is Wobar's second-largest register** — 16% of set runtime, 45% combined with dark/wonky 140 ([[reference/WOBAR_SONIC]] §6 table), and SONIC already notes the register is *"largely SoundCloud-native (uncleared flips can't be distributed)."* **The series is the content form of the lane correction**, not a departure from it.
4. **Instagram is actively rewarding serialization.** Meta's *Series* shipped June 2026 and its eligibility is circular — it goes to creators *already publishing serialized content* ([[working/RESEARCH_IG_MECHANICS]] §2.3–2.4). That file's own conclusion: **"serialize on plain Reels now."**

**And it does the §3.5 job that memes were the only candidate for.** §3.5 said if memes return, *"they come back as a format inside bucket 2, never as a fourth bucket."* A title that is a producer's joke, delivered on top of actual music, is that — the DM-travelling quality without the content-account risk, because the payload is a track.

**Where it does not go: originals.** [[working/WOBAR_GROWTH_PLAN]] §3 is unambiguous — uncleared flips cannot reach DSPs and are permanently non-monetisable. **Monthly listeners come from originals, full stop.** The series is marketing that drives to the back catalogue. **Do not let it become the release plan**, and do not score it on listeners.

### Bucket 3 — GIVE-BACK
Joe's job for it is **Credibility: interactive material that serves the community directly. This is where you differentiate.** The vault reached *differentiation* independently — that part of the two models agrees. **"Interactive" is Joe's word and is a sharper constraint than "artifact"** — it has not yet been tested against what follows.

> **⚠ The niche was decided 2026-08-04 and is owned by [[working/WOBAR_GIVEBACK_NICHE]], not by this file.** Locked there: audience is **DJ-producers who do both**, vocabulary is **tension and release**, thesis is **the mechanic never changes, only the scale does**, and content comes from a six-rung scale ladder. Evidence in [[working/RESEARCH_STORYTELLING]]. The candidate-artifact list below predates that decision and is now *one possible format* for the niche, not the strategy itself.

**Split in two. Nick's instinct to defer was right for one half and wrong for the other.**

| | Works when | Cost | Call |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Broadcast give-back** — patch packs, .tox releases, sample packs, tutorials | needs an audience to retain | real hours | **DEFER** |
| **1:1 give-back** — a visualizer made for one artist, a patch sent to one producer | **works at 55 followers** | near-free | **START NOW** |

**A gift from a nobody is memorable. A gift from a big artist is marketing. The 1:1 version is worth more today than it will ever be again.**

**Rule: give-back must be artifact-based, not advice-based.** Joe Rath's give-back menu is credentialed — production lessons, "who am I" vlogs, *"how to be an independent artist with no manager"* — and he can teach the path because he played EDC twice. Wobar has no result to teach from. **An artifact needs no credential: the tool either works or it does not.** *(The original line read "no result to teach from and no face." The face half was struck 2026-08-04 — see [[reference/WOBAR_WORLD]] §4. The rule stands on the credential half alone, which was always the load-bearing one.)*

**Candidate artifacts:** free TD/visualizer tools (.tox, GLSL, audio-reactive templates) · Serum patches ([[reference/WOBAR_PATCH_SYSTEM]]) · sample packs · free-DL flips (Acyan runs *"FLIPS (FREE DL)"*) · sound design **shown, not taught** (screen recording, no voice — obeys WORLD §8).

**The strongest single move, and it is not on Joe's list: make a visualizer for a rung-up artist's track and send it free.** They post it, they credit you, **your name lands inside their audience** — borrowed reach, the only kind that has ever worked here. Competence proof, rolodex and top-of-funnel in one object, in a skill nobody in dark-140 has.
*Cost control:* build one reusable rig and swap the audio. **This is the argument for the TouchDesigner investment that has been missing** — it makes TD compound instead of producing one-offs.

### How give-back combines with the world
**The world is the container, not the content.** The artifact does a plain useful thing; the world shows in its *form* — name, mark, palette, register — never in an explanation. `SURFACES`: **structure exposes more than prose.**

**And it supplies the missing acquisition path for wobar.music.** The terminal is the strongest thing in the project and by design has no traffic reason (it is *discovered*). A free tool is a legitimate reason to send someone there — they arrive for the .tox and are standing at depth 3.

### The thing that is not a bucket
**Behavior.** Comments, DMs, reposts, playing their tracks, flipping a rung-up artist's song, booking someone through Flow State.

**Content is the credential. Behavior is the reach.** No bucket contains it and it does more work than all three combined.

## §3.5 — MEMES: DEMOTED, NOT DELETED

Memes were bucket 2 in v1.0 and are not on Joe's list. **Demoted to an available format 2026-08-03 — not retired to [[working/WOBAR_CLOSED]], because nothing about it was disproved.** It lost a taxonomy argument, not an evidence one, and deleting the section would have destroyed two things worth keeping.

**What was true about it, and still is:**
- **Sends-per-reach is the strongest non-follower signal on Instagram** (VERIFIED, [[working/RESEARCH_IG_MECHANICS]] §1.2), and memes are the format that travels by DM.
- Cheap — minutes, not sessions.
- **Faceless-compatible.** No voice, no personality vector, no conflict with [[reference/WOBAR_WORLD]].
- **Zero credential required** — the only bucket-grade idea in the plan that needs no capture, no release and no result.

**Why that was uncomfortable:** it was the plan's only reach mechanism, and the bucket that replaced it at the top of the funnel was believed to be blocked — so the plan appeared to have no working top of funnel at all.

> **Resolved 2026-08-04.** Bucket 1 is unblocked (§3). **The argument that memes were the only available reach mechanism no longer holds**, and the revival condition below can now never fire.

**If it comes back, it comes back as a format inside bucket 2 or as a bounded test (§7), never as a fourth bucket.** ~~The condition that would justify reviving it: capture still parked and bucket 1 still empty three months out.~~ **Dead condition — bucket 1 is not empty.** The revival case would now have to be made on its own merits, not on bucket 1's absence.

### ⚠ THE STANDING RAIL — CUTDWN's documented failure mode

*Promoted out of the meme section 2026-08-03. It was never really about memes — it applies to **any** non-music content that outperforms the music.*

Joe Rath's breakout was a non-music viral reel (20M views, 1.5M likes, ~800K shares); followers went 5,000 → 20,000 and then, verbatim: *"My music posts after it pretty much flopped, because the new fanbase expected comedy skits. They did not see me as an artist, they saw me as a content creator."*

**This is the most valuable page in the playbook and it is the cautionary one.** Two fixes, both free, both applying plan-wide:

1. **Non-music content must signal competence, not be generally entertaining.** A joke only a dark-140 producer gets **is a credential**. A joke anyone could make is a content account. That line is the whole difference — and it governs give-back artifacts exactly as much as it governed memes.
2. **Pin the proof.** Three pinned posts, always music or performance. Whatever the feed does below, the top of the grid reads *producer* on inspection.

**⚠ Reweighted 2026-08-04.** Both fixes were written as cheap insurance while facelessness was a hard rule — the rule made "seen as a content creator" nearly impossible to trigger. **That rule is lifted ([[reference/WOBAR_WORLD]] §4), and bucket 1 puts a person in frame by definition.** These two are no longer precautions. They are the mechanism.

## §3.6 — THE LEGIBILITY CONSTRAINT (governs buckets 1 and 2)

*Promoted out of bucket 2 on 2026-08-04. It was never a visualizer problem — it is a **dark-on-black** problem, and bucket 1 shoots in the dark too.*

Visualizers pull 133–314 views. `elekktronaut` runs 45K followers on pure faceless abstract generative art at 4.9K–14.4K per Reel. **The category is not the problem.** His work is high-contrast, **one legible form per frame**, readable at thumbnail scale. Wobar's are dark-on-black full-frame texture fields — invisible as a thumbnail in a bright feed on a phone.

**The rule, stated once for both buckets: one legible high-contrast form per frame, tested at thumbnail size before it ships.**

| | The dark-on-black trap | What passes |
|---|---|---|
| **Bucket 2** — visualizers | full-frame texture field, no focal element | the vector-void's single focal element against true black *is* the compliant form — bright enough to survive downscaling |
| **Bucket 1** — live | wide crowd shot in a dark room | silhouette against a lighting wash — stage light is the one reliable contrast source in this bucket |
| **Bucket 1** — bedroom | flat evenly-lit room, no focal point | one lit element carrying the frame; a flat room is the same invisibility by a different route |

**This does not license brightening the brand.** The fix is *contrast and focal hierarchy*, not saturation or exposure. A muted frame with one legible form passes; a vivid frame with five does not.

## §4 — THE FUNNEL

```
PERFORMANCE ───►  reach, uncontrolled          ✅ UNBLOCKED 2026-08-04
   │               job: get seen by producers at all
   ▼
ENGAGEMENT ────►  they like / comment / follow
   │               job: they self-identify
   ▼
TRIAGE ────────►  YOU read the names, weekly
   │               job: find lane producers/DJs in the noise
   ▼
MUSIC / PROOF ─►  they check the grid
   │               job: NOT to persuade — to prevent dismissal
   ▼
WARM DM ───────►  you reach out, already known
   │               job: the conversion event
   ▼
SOUNDCLOUD ────►  they listen, download, maybe play it
```

**Performance gets attention. Music prevents dismissal. Behavior converts.**

**⚠ The "no mouth" finding is void as of 2026-08-04.** v1.1 read *"until capture exists, this funnel has no mouth"* — written on the belief that no footage existed. **A published 90-minute set and three named sources say otherwise (§3, bucket 1).** The funnel has an entry point.

**What replaces it is narrower and still true: the mouth exists but is not yet supplied on a cadence.** Live and recorded-set footage is ad hoc; the bedroom source is a habit that has not started. That is a supply problem, and supply problems are solved by doing the thing — which is exactly the class of blocker §7 warns gets converted into more planning.

### Three rules the diagram encodes

**1. The DM is outbound.** Waiting for a stranger to DM at 55 followers is waiting for nothing. **The funnel's only job is to make Nick's DM warm.** Joe Rath, same conclusion: *"I gave support months before I ever needed or asked for anything. By the time I reached out, I wasn't a cold name."*

**2. Triage is a step, and it is the one that will kill this.** Whatever runs at the top travels to producers *generally*, not to the target tier. The right people are found by **reading who engaged, not how many** — a weekly ten-minute habit. It is operational, not creative, which is exactly why it gets skipped.

**3. The destination is SoundCloud, not Spotify.** It is where DJs download, repost and play from, and the producer-follow is the native action there. **The DM links SoundCloud — not a smart link.** Spotify serves non-local fans arriving by other routes.

### Leaks, ranked by likelihood of killing it

| # | Leak | Fix | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Nobody notices who engaged** | The weekly triage habit | free |
| 2 | **Grid reads as a content account** | Three pinned proof posts | free |
| 3 | Post travels, profile never checked | Mark + handle legible in-frame | free |
| 4 | DM sent, no reply | Track quality. Nothing else fixes it | — |
| 5 | Listens once, never returns | Catalogue depth. Not solvable this year | — |

## §5 — WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WORKING

*A leading-metric table with counts at zero lived here and was removed 2026-07-30 — counting Nick's output back at him is tracking, not strategy.*

**Revised 2026-08-12.** v1.0 said *do not track IG followers, reach, views or engagement rate — aggregate engagement is the Insights trap.* **That prohibition is lifted.** Under §1 the account's size is the credibility mechanism, so refusing to measure it means refusing to measure the strategy.

**The trap it was guarding against is real and is now handled by scope instead of by blindness:** a number is only worth tracking if a decision hangs on it. Anything measured here that never changes what gets posted is decoration.

### The governing principle — yield, not volume

**Instagram runs on genuine surplus (§0 priority: music first, always). The lever of "post more" is closed.** So measurement exists to raise the return on a fixed and modest output, which means **the review must be as willing to say stop as to say do more.** A loop that only ever adds is not reading anything.

### Three tiers, and they answer different questions

| Tier | Reads | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Account strength** | Is the credential getting real? The §1 claim, directly | Followers · reach · per-post `Follows` · profile activity |
| **2 — Who** | Is it the *right* growth? The lane-weighting test | Which accounts comment, save, share, follow. Lane or not |
| **3 — Music signal** | Which work lands, per §1's second job | Reaction across finished-track posts, read over months |

**Tier 2 outranks tier 1 when they disagree.** One comment from a lane producer still outranks two hundred likes — that judgement survives v1.0 intact. A rising follower count made of non-lane accounts is the CUTDWN failure in progress (**§3.5, the standing rail**), not success, and tier 1 alone cannot see the difference.

**And the ratio, per §1's inversion:** IG growth is only credibility if SoundCloud moves with it. **Track the two together or the number is lying to you.**

### Still the strongest signals, unchanged
Reposts earned · confirmed DJ plays · unprompted replies or *"what is this"* from lane accounts · SoundCloud followers.

### ⚠ Why the cadence is not optional

**Account-level Instagram Insights is a rolling 90-day window and nothing survives it.** Presets are 7 / 14 / 30 days, previous month and 90 days; custom ranges must fall inside 90 days. Per-post and per-reel insights persist ~2 years, but the account-level series does not exist unless it is captured. **A biweekly snapshot is the only reason any of this data exists a year from now** — that, not analysis, is the load-bearing half of the habit.

Also: **audience demographics require a preset timeframe and >100 accounts reached or engaged.** At current size some windows will return nothing. That is a missing reading, not a result.

### Three honest limits
1. **This does not produce listeners directly.** It produces credibility, relationships and a read on the music. The listener arrives from what a peer does next, which is outside Nick's control. **Do not score it on streams.**
2. **The relationship half is slow.** Weeks to months per person. Fine across ~100 targets; not measurable fortnightly.
3. **The music-signal half is sample-starved** (§1). Direction over months is readable. Single-post verdicts mostly are not.

## §7 — WHAT THIS PLAN DOES NOT KNOW

1. **Whether producer-facing content produces distribution access.** The chain — content → producer respect → DM taken seriously → repost/play → their audience hears → some convert — is **six links, every one unmeasured. [JUDGMENT, unmeasured.]** A shorter chain (content → fan → listener) was just rejected for being unproven; this one is longer. Held because it is cheap with a fat tail, **not** because it is proven.
2. **Whether the peer layer is decoration on the only variable that matters.** Vulora: 16 posts, 119 monthly listeners, signed. If doors are taste-gated, content may change nothing. Untested and unfalsified.
3. ~~**Whether "cheap exhaust" is true.**~~ **Closed 2026-08-12 — by decision, not by measurement.** Nick enjoys making content and it is already part of how he shares music, so the cost is not a tax the strategy has to earn back. The residual risk is unchanged in kind but no longer decisive: **enjoyable work still consumes hours, and §0 is what bounds it.**
4. **The peer-tier comp set** (1K–20K) has never been built. Every comp is 45K+. **This now costs more than it did under v1.0** — a growth goal without a benchmark at this size means there is no external answer to *"is this number good?"*, only movement against Wobar's own past.

### The bounded test that would settle #1
**Ship 5 producer-facing posts over a month. Cap total time at 3 hours. Score one number: unprompted replies or DMs from lane accounts.** Zero after five and it is dead for three hours' cost. Two or three and it is real.

**Run it alongside the demos, never instead** — §0.

### The failure mode, named — and it applies harder to v2.0 than it did to v1.0
The vault's documented pattern is *"research feels like progress and is not"* — five sweeps, three rewrites, **0 demos sent.** A peer-layer strategy licenses content and TouchDesigner builds, which are the comfortable work, and requires nothing to be sent or judged.

**Test any expansion of this plan against one question: does it cause an uncomfortable action, or license a comfortable one?**

⚠ **Applied to the biweekly review itself, honestly:** a recurring analytics ritual is close to the archetype of comfortable work. It reads as progress, it is pleasant, and it can run for a year without a single demo being sent. **Three things keep it legitimate, and if any of them lapses the review is the failure mode wearing a spreadsheet:**

1. **It must change what gets posted.** A cycle that produces observations and no decision was a waste of the hour.
2. **It must be able to say stop** (§5, yield not volume). A review that only ever recommends more is not reading anything.
3. **It never moves ahead of the music** (§0).

**The honest reason it is still worth running:** the 90-day window means the data is destroyed if nobody captures it (§5). That much is true regardless of whether the analysis is any good.
