# WOBAR.lite

**What it is.** The same world, a different register — Acts 1 (RIFT) and 5 (INTEGRATION) of the 5-Act Portal Framework, isolated and made bookable on their own, without descending into the confrontational middle (2–4). Where core Wobar is dark, wonky 140, hypnotic and structured, WOBAR.lite is 80–120 BPM, organic, softer, more emotional. Same world. Different door in.

**Why it exists as a separate mark, not just a mood core Wobar sometimes has.** Practical, first — a separate mark on flyers and announcements distinguishes the two kinds of sets so nobody shows up expecting one and gets the other. The longer-arc version, in Nick's own words: three years out, headlining his own show, he can open for himself with a WOBAR.lite set — a genuinely different vibe, same artist, same night. It's a companion identity, not a competitor to core Wobar.

## Inherits from core Wobar, unchanged

- **The enemy — rushed tension and release.** Confirmed explicitly, and stronger than most of what carries over: *"the enemy definitely still applies here, and if anything, it's actually the reason for this to exist."* WOBAR.lite fights the same enemy at the gentler, more vulnerable ends of the arc instead of the confrontational middle.
- **The archetype pair — Magician + Creator.** Not re-litigated for this era. The Sage-toned voice already established as a separate axis from the strategic archetype (`reference/WOBAR_BRAND.md`, Brand Archetypes section) is available to WOBAR.lite the same way it's available to void poems and glimpses — a register choice, not a new archetype decision.
- **Same audience.** Confirmed: *"ideally the same crowd as core Wobar, but a different room, a different type of night, a different way to engage."* Not a new fanbase to build — a different door for the same people.

## What's different

- **Tempo:** 80–120 BPM, against core Wobar's dark/wonky 140 centre of mass.
- **Character:** organic instead of hypnotic and structured. Core Wobar's Act 3–4 territory is engineered, precise, confrontational; WOBAR.lite is felt, looser, warmer — closer to what Acts 1 and 5 already do inside a full Wobar set, expanded into their own space rather than bookending a descent.
- **Reference artists** (Nick's own, not yet cross-checked or expanded on): Skysha, Mfinity, Contra, Chimera. What pulled him toward this wasn't one transcendent set the way Tipper/Prydz anchored core Wobar's origin — it was noticing a body of sound he genuinely likes that doesn't fit his current dark/wonky production.
- **Production status:** not yet producing this. Currently a DJ/curation identity only. Nick's own read on where it's headed: *"I think this will eventually become production because it is acts one and five of my current sets that I am learning how to make"* — i.e., the production lane is the same material he already builds into the bookends of his own sets, not a new sound being invented from scratch.

## Relationship to Obscura (retired)

WOBAR.lite evolved out of Obscura's territory — Act 5, "the void made physical," the same-world-different-era mechanic — but deliberately does not inherit Obscura's specific design. Obscura's core musical instruction was "maximum hypnotic repetition"; WOBAR.lite is explicitly organic instead. Obscura was Act 5 only, built vertically; WOBAR.lite spans Acts 1 and 5. Full retirement narrative: `working/WOBAR_CLOSED.md`. Elements of Obscura's concrete design (the haze wall, the four-stage structure, the totems) are not currently part of WOBAR.lite, but were kept on disk rather than deleted specifically in case a future, more built-out production wants to pull them forward — Nick's call, not decided yet either way.

## Visual identity — what's confirmed

- **A separate wordmark exists**, distinct typeface from the primary WOBAR mark, in stacked/unstacked and black/white lockups. Not yet formally documented with rationale the way the primary mark is in `reference/WOBAR_BRAND.md` — asset files exist, the *why* behind the typeface choice hasn't been captured yet.
- Everything else visual (palette, whether it shares core Wobar's desaturated psychedelic range or shifts, texture, motion) is **open** — not yet decided, and shouldn't be assumed from Obscura's old visual language ("same palette, differently expressed, geometry still instead of moving") without Nick confirming that's still the direction.

## Genuinely open

- Full visual system beyond the wordmark.
- Whether WOBAR.lite gets its own explicit belief-system emphasis (which of the nine beliefs read differently in a softer context) or simply inherits all nine as-is — not yet tested the way core Wobar's beliefs were stress-tested.
- First real booking/performance as WOBAR.lite specifically — when this moves from "a set I could theoretically flyer differently" to an actual scheduled thing.
- Production timeline — when the first WOBAR.lite original gets made.
- Method going forward, per Nick's own framing at the start of this: **"we will iterate and pivot as needed and as we learn."** This document should get revised as that happens, not treated as a finished spec the way `reference/WOBAR_BRAND.md` now is.
