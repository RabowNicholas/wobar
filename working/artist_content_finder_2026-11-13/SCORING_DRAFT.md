# Scoring draft, 2026-11-16 (proposal, needs Nick's calibration before use)

The question: who uses TikTok and Instagram to drive streams. Followers alone don't answer it, so the score leans on reach and habit.

## Gates (already set by Nick, 2026-11-13)
- Spotify monthly listeners 10k to 100k (main) or 100k to 500k (stretch, reference only).
- 10k+ followers on TikTok or Instagram.
- Lane: core or adjacent, tagged separately. 20 off-lane flags from the identity step get confirmed here.

## Proposed score, 0 to 100 (read from the last 12 posts or videos on each platform)
| Signal | Points | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Median views per video, best platform | 35 | reach is what moves streams |
| Reach ratio: median views / followers | 25 | content travelling past the existing fanbase |
| Cadence: posts in the last 30 days | 15 | a habit, not a lucky post |
| Music share: posts built on their own track | 15 | content as a release tool, not lifestyle |
| Active TikTok (posted in last 30 days) | 10 | TikTok is where discovery happens |

Each signal is ranked within the roster (percentile), so no fixed thresholds to argue about.

## Calibration ask (same as the label run)
Nick names 3 to 5 artists he knows drive streams with content, and 2 or 3 who don't despite big follower counts. If the score doesn't sort them right, the weights change before ranking.

## Cost
Step 3 reads the last 12 posts per artist on each platform. Only artists past the gates get read (est. 350 to 450).
