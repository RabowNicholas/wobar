## Patterns (top 27 Instagram artists, 398 posts read, 324 covers read by eye)

1. **A text hook on the cover is the default.** 12 of 27 put a hook line on most reels. The line names a feeling or a situation, not the song: "How do I explain to a normal person that I find this type of music incredibly soothing" (370k), "lost lands really had me djing on expert mode" (692k), "ok but what would you do if i play this at Lost Lands?" (254k).
2. **Promo is the floor.** The lowest post in an account is very often the flyer, the ad, or the "OUT NOW" post: The Resistance flyer, Dr. Ushuu sample-pack ad, Stoned Level masterclass ad (3.3k, his worst), AKIRAH "OUT NOW", KARAN! flyer. In captions, "out now / link in bio" appears in 7% of breakout posts vs 14% of the rest.
3. **Unreleased "ID" is a hook.** Posts whose audio is labelled "Artist - ID" have a median of 51k vs 26k for everything else (16 posts, small sample). Covers use it directly: "POV: you just stumbled onto another chibs ID".
4. **Recurring bits with a fixed template.** "WHAT IN THE 2013 DUBSTEP IS THIS" (128k), the "Skalavengers" series, Space Wizard's "wizard council", HAMRO's fan-on-stage. A template lets the audience recognise the post in the first second.
5. **Persona and world beat plain footage.** Space Wizard (cloaked wizard + lore), The Resistance (masked collective), Saxsquatch (costume). The persona does the hook work that a face does for others.
6. **Faceless works when the world is strong.** The Resistance hits 12 of 12 with masks, one grade and no text at all. Nimda / yvm3 and Sharks also run on a dark, consistent look.
7. **Producers win with process on screen.** Webcam + DAW split (MUERTE 722k and 257k, Stoned Level), DAW how-to (MOONBOY), wireframe visualizer split (Chibs).
8. **Milestone shows spike.** Lost Lands posts are the biggest for 2DY4 (692k, 267k), Space Wizard (117k) and Dr. Ushuu (254k). The hook frames the show as a personal moment, not an announcement.
9. **Collabs carry reach.** 27% of the posts read live on someone else's account (collab or feature). Nimda's and yvm3's top reels are largely shared collab posts.

## Tests for Nick's songs (hypotheses, pick and run; each maps to a pattern above)
1. **Wireframe split** (pattern 7, Chibs model): top half a WOBAR vector-void render driven by the drop, bottom half Nick at the desk reacting. One lowercase hook line. Fits "face allowed, personality not sold".
2. **In-world hook line on every cover** (patterns 1, 5, Space Wizard model): a fixed lowercase voice that speaks from inside the world, never explaining the center.
3. **Faceless cinematic live POV** (pattern 6, Resistance model): show footage, one heavy grade, zero text. For gig content only.
4. **"wobar - ID" posts** (pattern 3): label the audio as an ID, hook on the moment of discovery.
5. **Never lead with promo** (pattern 2): release info goes in the caption, the video leads with the moment.
6. **One recurring bit** (pattern 4): one template line reused weekly, e.g. a rap-flip reveal series ("what if [song] was 140"), matched to the lane (dark/wonky 140 + rap flips).
7. **Collab-post with peers** (pattern 9): use Instagram collab posts with the crew on shared IDs and b2bs.

## Limits
Views are as shown on the grid at read time. Covers were read by eye; the first frame of the video can differ from the cover. "Which song section" is not filled in (no audio). Breakout tags from captions are weak on their own; the cover read carries most of the signal.
