## 4. Takeaways for learning

- **The working conclusion holds: nobody in the core lane publishes source material.** None of the wonky/dark 140, rap-flip or dub artists had packs, presets, stems or project files on Bandcamp, Splice or their bios. Study their sound by ear and by recreation. Don't wait for a pack.
- **The two closest-lane exceptions are the best buys.** **Daggz** sells Serum bass presets ($10–13, dubstep, neuro, psychedelic) and books 1:1 lessons. That is the nearest thing to a teacher in the psy/dark 140 register. **Comisar** (deep/hypnotic) runs an active Patreon with packs and Serum presets. Open the presets and reverse-engineer the patches. Don't just play them.
- **Stems teach more than one-shots.** Shlump's free remix pack and Ternion Sound's $7 Sway stems show finished-track layering, bus decisions and arrangement density. One-shot packs can't.
- **Tsuruda 1–6 for $15 and Eprom Emotion Generator for $30 are the cheapest way to hear the wonky drum and bass palette raw.** Eprom's pack is the closest you can get to the G Jones/Eprom sound world, since G Jones has released nothing.
- **Splice credits are already paid for.** Pull CloZee, Subtronics, Virtual Riot, Leotrix, Ternion, Stylust and Mr. Bill Basses for A/B reference on sub weight, transient shape and bass layering. Mr. Bill's $20/mo membership is the only route to many real project files. Take it for a month only if you'll actually open the projects.
