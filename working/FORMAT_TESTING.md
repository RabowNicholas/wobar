## SAMPLES I'LL NEVER GET CLEARED

**Status:** named 2026-08-04 (Nick). Unshipped.
**Bucket:** 2 — Music ([[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §3).
**Funnel:** C — Flips, THE TRAFFIC ([[working/WOBAR_ROADMAP]] §C).

### What it is
A numbered flip series. The name is the frame: each entry is a flip of something that could never be licensed, and the series says so out loud.

**It closes a standing instruction.** [[working/WOBAR_GROWTH_PLAN]] §2 has read ***"Name it, number it, keep it running"*** since 2026-07-26, with no name attached. This is the name.

**No collision with *Portal Journey*.** That is the mix/set series (currently at 002). **Portal Journey = sets. Samples = flips.** Two numbered series on one main handle is the observed norm in this lane, not a conflict — Tape B runs *Vibe Check* and *Driptapes* concurrently on his verified account.

### Why the premise is safe
The title sounds like a confession and is not. **Tape B's *Vibe Check Vol. 3* is openly uncleared major-label material** — Mariah Carey, Lady Gaga, Destiny's Child, t.A.T.u. — published on his verified main handle. GorillaT runs *Gorilla Tapes* the same way. **The practice is the scene norm; naming it is the only novel part.**

Already settled elsewhere and not re-litigated here: flips are permanently off DSPs and permanently non-monetisable ([[working/WOBAR_GROWTH_PLAN]] §3), and flips are the designated publishable lane so they do not burn label exclusivity ([[working/WOBAR_ACTIVE]]).

### The two-beat unit — the format's actual mechanic
**The title promises a reveal, so the unit has to deliver one.** The interesting object is not the flip; it is the distance between the source and what Nick did to it.

```
beat 1   the sample, recognisable          ← the borrowed attention
beat 2   what it became                    ← the credential
```

**A clip that is only beat 2 is a visualizer with a funny title.** If the source is not legible in the clip, the series name is doing no work and the format has collapsed into a normal flip post.

**This is the same borrowed-reach mechanic the plan already runs on** — §1's finding that *"the only time Wobar content ever reached anyone, another account carried it."* Here the carrier is a recognisable song rather than a collaborator. **Consequence: the flip has to survive direct comparison with the original inside a single clip.** That is a real bar and it is the format's main risk of embarrassment.

### Constraints it inherits
- **§3.6 legibility** — a DAW screen recording passes this easily, and the vault has in-lane evidence: `@vanda.wav` runs DAW screens and artwork faceless at ~145% views-per-follower; `@coido__` runs hardware jams at ~124% ([[working/RESEARCH_IG_COMPS]]). **This is the format the comps say works, done by the artists closest to Wobar's size.**
- **§3.5 standing rail** — competence, not general entertainment. See the open question below, because the title only half-passes it.
- **WORLD §8 — shown, never explained.** Screen recording, no voice, no tutorial. A flip taken apart on camera is a demonstration; a flip explained is a lesson, and Wobar has no result to teach from.

### Open questions
1. **Does the name cover both surfaces, or only SoundCloud?** The strongest read is one name across both — the SoundCloud numbered series is the release container, the IG clips are its promotion, exactly as *Driptapes* works. **Not yet decided.** If it is SoundCloud-only, the IG clips need their own frame and the serialization argument weakens.
2. **The title is industry-insider, not lane-insider.** §3.5's rail distinguishes *"a joke only a dark-140 producer gets"* from *"a joke anyone could make."* Sample clearance is legible to any music person. **The title gets it in the door; the sample choices are what make it a credential.** A Mariah flip and a rap flip in Wobar's actual register say very different things about who this is for. **The lane specificity has to live in the selections, because the name does not supply it.**
3. **The name forecloses the clearable case.** A flip of an indie artist, a friend, or anything licensable cannot live in a series premised on never clearing. Small, but it means the series can never graduate into a release — every entry is terminal by design. That is consistent with Funnel C and worth knowing rather than discovering.
4. **Numbering scheme undecided** — volumes (Tape B) vs single increments (*Portal Journey 002*).

### Kill condition
**Per [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §5 this is not scored on views.** The signal is who: unprompted replies, ID requests, or reposts from lane accounts, and SoundCloud follows.

**Kill it if six entries produce no lane-account response.** Six is enough to be a series rather than a test, and it caps the loss. Note the honest limit — flips out-traffic originals ~4× on Tape B's account, but **Tape B has an audience and Wobar does not**; the mechanism may not transfer at 55 SC followers. Nothing in the vault tests it at this scale.

**The trap to watch, named in advance:** this is a comfortable format. It licenses making flips, which is studio work Nick enjoys, and it produces no uncomfortable action. Per §7 it must run *alongside* the demos, never instead of them.
