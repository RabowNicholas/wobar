## §1 — THE DEPTH LADDER

`WORLD` §7 models this as binary — *seen world* above, *the underneath* below, one threshold between. **That is too coarse.** It has one door; most surfaces never get near it.

| Depth | What's exposed | Who's there | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — The sound** | nothing but music. No world | strangers, algorithms, A&Rs, DJs | a track exists and hits |
| **1 — The shape** | something is *structured*. An arc, a recurring mark, artwork that rhymes | repeat listeners, followers | **structure and image only — never words** |
| **2 — The voice** | Wobar as a presence, not just output | fans | journal register |
| **3 — The language** | the world's own vocabulary. Cosmology visible as a *thing* | Wanderers | source register, figurative licence |
| **4 — The centre** | the Mirror | nobody arrives — it is **glimpsed** | shown, never explained (§8) |

### Every surface, placed
**Operates** = where it sits now. **Ceiling** = the deepest it could ever go.

| Surface | Operates | Ceiling | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beatport / labels | — | **0** | Structurally cannot hold world. Metadata and a title |
| DJ plays / VA credits | 0 | **0** | Someone else's context entirely |
| Reddit | 0 | **0 by choice** | World does not show. **Competence is the exposure** |
| Instagram | 0 | 1 | **Audience is peers, not fans** — see [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]]. Ceiling was never the problem; the surface cannot make a fan in 3 seconds |
| TikTok | — | 1 | Crosspost of the above. Never designed for |
| Spotify | 0 | **1** | Canvas is depth-1: shape, wordless |
| SoundCloud | 0–1 | **2** | Catalogue + flip series = shape. Descriptions could carry voice |
| Release artwork | 1 | **1** | Pure shape, never words. **Highest-frequency depth-1 surface** |
| YouTube | 1 | **2** | Chaptered sets = shape. About = voice |
| Flow State / live | 1 | **3** | Bodies in a room. Deepest possible physical surface. Undesigned |
| SMS / Mirror | — | **3** | The door. Crossing is the act |
| **wobar.music (terminal)** | **3** | **3** | **LIVE.** Where the language lives openly |
| The Passage | — | **3** | Deepest *written* form |
| The mark | 1 | **1** | Cross-cutting, wordless, no register risk |
| **The music** | 0 | **4** | **The only surface with a ceiling of 4.** Can show the centre and structurally cannot explain it |

### What the map shows
1. **Almost everything operates at depth 0**, with ceilings of 0–1. That is what those surfaces *are*. The mistake is pushing world into them.
2. **Depth 1 is the entire mid-range and it is almost unbuilt** — artwork, Canvas, chaptered sets, the mark recurring. Shape and image, no words. Cheapest depth to build, least built.
3. **There is nothing between depth 1 and depth 3.** Depth 2 — Wobar as a voice — has no home. IG stories were meant to hold it; IG is demoted. SMS holds it and does not exist yet. **Someone who loves the music has nowhere to meet the person.**
4. **The music reaches 4 without a single word.** Why the vault says the audio was always closest to the world.

### The pre/post-experience test — apply to any new surface
**SoundCloud's bio is read *before* listening** (an A&R deciding whether to press play) → utility, five seconds, world is friction.
**YouTube's About is read *after*** (someone who watched, asking what that was) → world belongs.
Same artifact type, opposite answers.

### Structure exposes more than prose
A **chaptered set** makes the five-act arc visible without a word about it. The **Portal EP** *is* the arc made audible. **Structural exposure never violates §8** — it shows and cannot tell.

## §2 — THE BORROWED SURFACES (added 2026-07-27)

A category the map was missing entirely. The world also appears **inside someone else's frame**, where presentation isn't controlled:

- **Flow State's channels** — the set went out as *"Wobar and Flow State"*
- **A DJ playing the track** — funnel D's payoff happens on someone else's stage
- **A label's release page** — a VA placement puts Wobar inside Subsidia's or DUPLOC's world
- **A compilation tracklist** — one name among 29

**No copy can be written for these.** What's handed over determines what shows: **title, artwork, artist name, metadata.** This is why the titles-and-artwork decisions carry more weight than they appear to.
