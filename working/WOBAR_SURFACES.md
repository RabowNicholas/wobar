# SURFACES — DEPTH MAP & EXPOSURE STATUS

> There is a world underneath the project. It is the backbone and the reason.
> Surfaces are **membranes onto it** — each exposes a different audience to a
> different depth. This file maps that, and records what is deployed where.
>

## §1 — THE DEPTH LADDER

`WORLD` §7 models this as binary — *seen world* above, *the underneath* below, one threshold between. **That is too coarse.** It has one door; most surfaces never get near it.

| Depth | What's exposed | Who's there | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — The sound** | nothing but music. No world | strangers, algorithms, A&Rs, DJs | a track exists and hits |
| **1 — The shape** | something is *structured*. An arc, a recurring mark, artwork that rhymes | repeat listeners, followers | **structure and image only — never words** |
| **2 — The voice** | Wobar as a presence, not just output | fans | journal register |
| **3 — The language** | the world's own vocabulary. Cosmology visible as a *thing* | Wanderers | source register, figurative licence |
| **4 — The centre** | the Mirror | nobody arrives — it is **glimpsed** | shown, never explained (§8) |

### Every surface, placed
**Operates** = where it sits now. **Ceiling** = the deepest it could ever go.

| Surface | Operates | Ceiling | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beatport / labels | — | **0** | Structurally cannot hold world. Metadata and a title |
| DJ plays / VA credits | 0 | **0** | Someone else's context entirely |
| Reddit | 0 | **0 by choice** | World does not show. **Competence is the exposure** |
| Instagram | 0 | 1 | **Audience is lane-weighted — peers first, but growth is now a goal** ([[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §1, revised 2026-08-12). Ceiling was never the problem; the surface cannot make a fan in 3 seconds — **and no longer needs to.** Its jobs are credibility and reading the music, neither of which this axis measures |
| TikTok | — | 1 | Crosspost of the above. Never designed for |
| Spotify | 0 | **1** | Canvas is depth-1: shape, wordless |
| SoundCloud | 0–1 | **2** | Catalogue + flip series = shape. Descriptions could carry voice |
| Release artwork | 1 | **1** | Pure shape, never words. **Highest-frequency depth-1 surface** |
| YouTube | 1 | **2** | Chaptered sets = shape. About = voice |
| Flow State / live | 1 | **3** | Bodies in a room. Deepest possible physical surface. Undesigned |
| SMS / Mirror | — | **3** | The door. Crossing is the act |
| **wobar.music (terminal)** | **3** | **3** | **LIVE.** Where the language lives openly |
| The Passage | — | **3** | Deepest *written* form |
| The mark | 1 | **1** | Cross-cutting, wordless, no register risk |
| **The music** | 0 | **4** | **The only surface with a ceiling of 4.** Can show the centre and structurally cannot explain it |

### What the map shows
1. **Almost everything operates at depth 0**, with ceilings of 0–1. That is what those surfaces *are*. The mistake is pushing world into them.
2. **Depth 1 is the entire mid-range and it is almost unbuilt** — artwork, Canvas, chaptered sets, the mark recurring. Shape and image, no words. Cheapest depth to build, least built.
3. **There is nothing between depth 1 and depth 3.** Depth 2 — Wobar as a voice — has no home. IG stories were meant to hold it; ~~IG is demoted~~ **IG is no longer demoted (2026-08-12), but that does not fix this** — its assigned jobs are credibility and reading the music, not carrying voice, and its ceiling is still 1. SMS holds depth 2 and does not exist yet. **Someone who loves the music still has nowhere to meet the person.**
4. **The music reaches 4 without a single word.** Why the vault says the audio was always closest to the world.

### §1.5 — THE SECOND AXIS: DURATION HELD

Added 2026-07-30. Depth answers *how much world is exposed*. It does not answer *whether anyone comes back* — and under [[working/WOBAR_ROADMAP]] §1 the goal is **people who come back**. **Fans are made by duration, not reach.**

| Surface | Holds someone for | Return power |
|---|---|---|
| **Flow State / live set** | **60–90 min, undivided** | **Highest in the project** |
| A mix / chaptered set | 20–60 min | High |
| Full visualizer / YouTube set | 4–90 min, and **evergreen in search** | High, and it does not expire |
| A track | ~4 min | Real |
| Release artwork · Canvas | seconds, but **repeated every play** | Low each, compounding |
| Instagram / TikTok clip | **3 seconds to the decision** | **Structurally cannot make a fan** |

**What this axis shows that the depth ladder could not:**

1. **Live is the strongest surface on both axes** — ceiling 3 on depth, highest duration in the project — **and it is the one marked *undesigned*.** That is now the largest single gap in this file.
2. **An unfilmed set converts only the bodies present.** Capture is what lets a 90-minute surface exist anywhere else. Listed in §4 as a missing surface; it is really a missing *habit*.
3. **Three seconds cannot produce a return, whatever the content is.** That finding stands. ⚠ **What it was used for has changed (2026-08-12):** it read *"Instagram's demotion is structural, not a judgment."* **Instagram is no longer demoted** — [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §1 makes it a surface in its own right with a growth goal. **The two axes in this file measure depth of world and duration held. Instagram's actual jobs — credibility with peers, and reading reaction to the music — are invisible to both.** A surface can score 0/1 here and still be worth building. This file no longer speaks to Instagram's priority.
4. **YouTube is undervalued everywhere in this vault.** It is the only long-form surface with a ceiling above 1, and search keeps it alive for years while a post dies in 48 hours.
5. **Depth 2 is where the return leaks.** *"Someone who loves the music has nowhere to meet the person."* Under a listener goal that was a curiosity; under a return goal it is the hole.

**Corroboration, inside one account:** Swomp's numbered mix series pulls **36.3K plays**; his most recent single track pulls **1,232**. **30×.** Long-form wins because duration *is* the mechanism.

### The pre/post-experience test — apply to any new surface
**SoundCloud's bio is read *before* listening** (an A&R deciding whether to press play) → utility, five seconds, world is friction.
**YouTube's About is read *after*** (someone who watched, asking what that was) → world belongs.
Same artifact type, opposite answers.

### Structure exposes more than prose
A **chaptered set** makes the five-act arc visible without a word about it. The **Portal EP** *is* the arc made audible. **Structural exposure never violates §8** — it shows and cannot tell.

### §3.2 — CLAIM THE SCALE THE SURFACE DELIVERS

*Added 2026-08-07. Derived from §1.5 and now governing what copy may assert where.*

> **A claim pitched above the duration a surface delivers reads as a promise the surface cannot keep.**

Spotify is consumed as **standalone tracks** — §1.5 already says a track holds ~4 minutes — so a Spotify bio asserting a 90-minute arc is arguing with the platform. **This does not weaken the arc thesis; it locates it.** [[working/WOBAR_GIVEBACK_NICHE]] belief 1 already resolves the tension: *the mechanic never changes, only the scale does.* A four-minute track is the arc at a lower rung, not a compromised version of it.

| Surface | Unit consumed | What copy may claim |
|---|---|---|
| Spotify | one track | what a track does to you |
| SoundCloud | track + mix | both |
| **YouTube** | 20–90 min | **the arc — this is its home** |
| Live | a night | the whole thing |
| A released EP | ~20 min in order | the arc returns to the DSPs |

**The arc language belongs on YouTube and was found there**, already written, in the 99-min set's description. It does not belong on Spotify until an EP exists — and there is no EP; the catalogue is three singles ([[working/WOBAR_ROADMAP]] §4's catalogue gap, made concrete).

## §6 — WOBAR.MUSIC — WHAT THE TERMINAL IS FOR

*Added 2026-08-07. Numbered §5 on creation, colliding with the existing §5 — renumbered §6 same day; nothing linked to it. Strategy only. Form and mechanics stay in [[working/WEB_HOME_SPEC]]; surface **role** stays in [[reference/WOBAR_WORLD]] §7.5. This section owns **what job it does in the system**.*

### The property nothing else has

**It is the only always-available depth-3 surface.** Live also reaches 3 but needs a body in a room in SLC on one night a month. SMS reaches 3 and does not exist. Everything else is capped at 0–2 and no amount of time raises it.

> **The terminal is the only surface where time spent increases depth.**

A track gives everyone the same four minutes. A set gives ninety. The terminal gives whatever someone chooses to spend and rewards more of it. Under [[working/WOBAR_ROADMAP]] §1 — people who come back — and §1.5 — *fans are made by duration, not reach* — **that is the most valuable single property in this file.**

⚠ **Corrected 2026-08-07 — that property is designed for, not built.** Audited against the deployed code (`wobar-landing-page` @ `9c03a45`). The claim above survives as the **target**; it is not a description of the artifact, and it was load-bearing in this section while being false.

The bank is finite and fully enumerable: **3 passages · 30 wander fragments · 9 deflection entries** — ~43 authored responses. Selection is `Math.random()` **with replacement**, and the terminal holds **no state whatsoever** — no `localStorage`, no cookie, no session memory. Nothing accumulates, nothing unlocks, nothing is withheld for later.

The consequences are arithmetic, not opinion. `poem` has three entries, so a second `poem` repeats one time in three. By a fifteenth `wander` a visitor has seen ~12 of the 30 and hit ~3 repeats. **A repeat is the surface announcing its own ceiling** — it teaches the limit before it has delivered the content. `menu` shows the entire surface area in four lines, one of which is `clear`. Minute 1 and minute 10 return the same class of object, and a second visit is identical to the first.

**What is genuinely depth 3 is the register — the voice, the deflection, the refusal to answer. The structure is flat.** Duration is the correct thing to aim at and the thing to build next *in the terminal*, whenever the terminal is next worked on — it is not a property that can be cited as already held.

**Error class: a spec'd intention read as a deployed fact** — the same shape as the 2026-08-07 trap in [[working/WOBAR_ACTIVE]] STANDING TRAPS, and it recurred inside a day of that trap being written down.

### The one job

**Convert people who already care into people who belong.** Nothing upstream of that.

**It is not an acquisition surface and must never be measured as one.** Traffic to wobar.music is a vanity number, and **a stranger arriving is a routing failure, not a success** (§3.3).

**The one metric that means anything: `found` Wanderers.** `source: invited | found` — `invited` is Nick doing outreach; **`found` is the terminal working.**

⚠ **Corrected 2026-08-07 — the field is specified, not built.** This said *"[[working/MIRROR_THRESHOLD_SPEC]] §0 already built the field."* §0 **specifies** it; the deployed schema does not have it. `lib/db/schema.sql` is `subscribers(id, phone, opted_in, opted_out_at, created_at)` — no `source` column — and the project carries **no analytics of any kind**.

So `found` is not merely zero pending the door: **it is unmeasurable, and would stay unmeasurable if the Mirror shipped tomorrow.** Return visits — the second success signal for this surface — are likewise unrecorded. **Two of the three signals this section names have no instrument.** The column is one line of DDL and has to land with the Mirror build, not after it.

Not: sessions, bounce rate, time-on-page as a headline, or anything Instagram sends it.

### Three prohibitions

- **Never carries a genre word.** It is the pure *identity* slot ([[reference/WOBAR_COPY]] §THE THREE SLOTS) — no retrieval value, no recipient to register-match against. Accuracy and world decide, and nothing else has a claim.
- **Never advertised.** Discovered, per its own spec. A CTA pointing at it converts the thing into a landing page.
- **Never the front door.** See §3.3, which is live and wrong right now.

**Scope clarified 2026-08-07 (Nick).** *Never advertised* governs **inbound** CTAs — anything pointing *at* `wobar.music`. It does **not** govern the terminal's own outbound action pointing at the music. The prohibition stands unamended; what follows is deployed under it, not against it.

### Deployed 2026-08-07 — `listen` as the primary action

`listen` was a typed command behind `menu`. It is now a bracketed **`[ listen ]` button** under the mark, above the daemon's first line, rendered before the intro finishes typing. The platform pick renders as buttons too, so **the whole path from arrival to the music completes without a keyboard**. Taps route through the same handler as typed input, so the transcript records a tap identically to a typed command and the terminal stays a record of what you did. **SoundCloud now leads the platform order** (§3.3 — SoundCloud is the mouth). Listen mode accepts a platform name as well as its number. Commit `9c03a45`.

**Reasoning (Nick's call).** While §3.3 stays live, arrivals here are disproportionately strangers, and the room offered them nothing reachable without tapping the screen, waiting for a keyboard and typing six characters. Bracketed rather than filled so it reads as a terminal token, not a marketing CTA. It sits in the scroll flow rather than pinned: seen on arrival, gone once someone is in — pinning would nag Wanderers permanently.

**The cost, recorded so it is not lost:** the most prominent action in a room whose stated metric is duration and return is now an **exit**. That tension was accepted deliberately, and it is the second reason §3.3 is worth fixing at the source — the button is triage for a routing defect, and its justification weakens the moment Instagram stops pointing here.

### The honest problem: it was built top-down

**The qualified population for a depth-3 room is currently 63 SoundCloud followers, 19 monthly listeners, and whoever was in the room at Flow State.** The terminal is not underperforming — **almost nobody is eligible to walk in.**

Depth 1 is this file's *"emptiest and cheapest band."* Depth 2 has no home at all and is where §1.5 says the return leaks. **So the next work is depth 1 and 2, not depth 3 — and the terminal's value rises automatically as those fill, without touching it.**

**One thing that follows and is easy to miss:** the daemon is a *process*, not Nick. **A finished terminal still does not close the depth-2 gap.** If depth 2 is where the return leaks, the terminal cannot be the thing that fixes it.

### What it already earns, unnamed until now

**Credibility through instantiation.** Every artist claims a world; Wobar has one that can be handed to someone. To a booker, an A&R, or a peer at 2K–20K deciding whether this is serious, a working terminal is evidence the project is not a logo and a Serum preset.

**That value is real at 19 monthly listeners, requires no funnel, and is already paid for.** It is the argument for the terminal existing *now* rather than after the middle of the map is built.
