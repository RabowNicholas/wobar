# GIVE-BACK CONTENT PLAN

Fifteen pieces across five lists. Derived from Nick's own observation, not from the research — [[working/RESEARCH_BRAND_PSYCHOLOGY]] cleared the ground (killed the archetype lane, killed the education frame, proved the space occupied) but generated none of the takes.

---

## §1 — THE TWO RULES THAT GOVERN EVERY PIECE

**1. Duration is not length — it is what kind of claim you are allowed to make.**

| | What it can carry |
|---|---|
| **15s** | One assertion, zero evidence. **A provocation only** — it can't prove, only make someone want the proof |
| **30s** | Assertion + one piece of evidence. One example, no argument |
| **45s** | Assertion + evidence + **a turn**. Room for one reversal |
| **61s** | Three beats and a conclusion. A real small argument |
| **90s** | A full case — setup, evidence, complication, landing |

A 15s piece and a 90s piece on the same idea are **different genres, not a cut-down**. Each piece below is assigned the duration it is native to.

**2. Every piece needs an object on screen** ([[working/WOBAR_GIVEBACK_NICHE]] §5.1). Under the criticism frame the object can be **anyone's** work — it does not have to be Nick's, which is what unblocks all of this before any set is captured.

⚠ **Two pieces below have no natural object** (#3 and #10) — they are pure lines. They are also two of the best lines in the set. Solve the object before shooting, or they default to talking-head, which is the register that measurably scores zero.

## §2 — THE STANDING CONSTRAINTS

- **Negative examples must be big enough to take it.** Using a small artist's inconsistent covers is punching down at the exact peer audience this bucket serves. Large artists, anonymised, or Nick's own back catalogue — the last is strongest, because self-criticism is immune to the credentials objection.
- **Check every number.** "Zeds Dead, 30 years" is wrong — it is roughly fifteen. The whole credibility play is *accuracy in a field of vibes*; casual figures are the cheapest way to lose it, and it is the vault's own standing trap.
- **Concede the enemy's case first.** The strongest rhetorical structure in the set (#1) grants that posting more works, in full, before attacking on a different axis. Use it wherever it fits — it disarms, where flat contradiction makes people defensive.

## §3 — LIST 1: CONSISTENCY — FREQUENCY VS CHARACTER

*Belief 2. The flagship list.*

| # | Piece | Claim | Object on screen | Native |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The concession** | **1,000** videos at 1,000 views *is* a million views — granted in full. Frequency buys attention; character decides whether it accumulates or evaporates. **A million views that don't compound is a million you have to earn again tomorrow.** ⚠ The raw version said 10,000 × 1,000 = a million; that is ten million, and 10,000 clips is 27 years of daily posting. 1,000 is correct *and* realistic | The arithmetic, over a real artist page — dense grid, flat numbers, beside one with a single spike | **61s** |
| **2** | **Whose box** | The viral artist got boxed in by an *accident* — the algorithm chose their box and now they serve it. The consistent artist chose theirs. Same cage, opposite ownership. | Two pages side by side | **45s** |
| **3** | **The two consistencies** | *Showing up consistently* vs *being consistent when you show up.* Everyone teaches the first. The second is the one that works. | ⚠ **unsolved** — a posting calendar against a visual grid? | **15s** |

**Note:** #2 resolves a contradiction that existed in the raw material — the box read as a trap in this list and as freedom in list 3. The reconciliation is ownership, and it is backed by the one piece of hard evidence in the research: **externally imposed constraints damage creativity; the identical constraint self-imposed does not** (Acar, Tarakci & van Knippenberg, *J. Management* 2019, 145 studies).

**Killed:** *"if it's not building momentum, don't do it"* as a rule. Good line, unusable — you cannot know in advance. Replaced by the checkable version: **does this repeat something?**

## §4 — LIST 2: CONTRAST AXES

*Belief 1 extended. Bar → track → set → release → catalogue → genre → field.*

| # | Piece | Claim | Object on screen | Native |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **4** | **Post Malone** | Metal → singer-songwriter → rap → country. Looked wildly inconsistent; was completely consistent **below genre**. *"In reality he was always one thing."* Genre was the variable, the person was the constant. | The discography and era imagery in sequence | **90s** |
| **5** | **The field is a gift** | Everyone chasing the one artist at the top, every label wanting one sound — usually described as the problem. It is the **stable reference that makes you legible as a move.** Contrast needs something that isn't moving, and the field is holding very still. | A wall of near-identical cover art from one lane | **45s** |
| **6** | **The pioneer's trap** | The consistency that made you a pioneer is what later makes you boring. Same property, opposite verdict, separated only by time. | One artist's catalogue laid out chronologically | **61s** |

**Why #4 opens the series:** it demonstrates the loop exactly, it is mainstream so it works on someone who has never heard of Rezz, and it is **outside electronic music** — which pre-empts *"that's a dance music thing"* before it is said.

**Held for later, noticed but undeveloped:** releases targeted at different parts of a set, different festival stages, core vs new listener. That is belief 2's depth ladder applied to a catalogue — a bridge between the two beliefs on a new object.

## §5 — LIST 3: THE FREEDOM ARGUMENT

*Self-defined boxes. The list that makes belief 2 legible to producers.*

| # | Piece | Claim | Object on screen | Native |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **7** | **The blank DAW** | No container, so you make whatever is there — and it is surface-level, because you never went deep enough on one thing. **The container is a production tool before it is an identity.** | An empty project | **30s** |
| **8** | **Year two vs year twenty** | You are comparing yourself to someone with fifteen more years of accumulated range. They can do every style because they spent two decades earning each one. | Two discographies side by side | **30s** |
| **9** | **You built it, so you can move it — but it costs** | Change is allowed and rationed. **One reset is a rebrand. Three is a personality.** | An identity changing across releases — Overmono, dogs installed 2020 | **45s** |

**Why #7 matters most strategically:** the audience does not want a brand. They want the blank project to stop being blank. Framing the container as a *production* tool smuggles identity thinking into a production audience before they notice they are being told about identity.

**#9 exists to close an escape hatch.** *"You created the world so you can change it whenever you want"* voids the argument — an at-will constraint is not a constraint, and that is precisely the unfalsifiability that killed the archetype lane. The rationed version keeps it honest.

## §6 — LIST 4: THE MYTHS

| # | Piece | Claim | Object on screen | Native |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **10** | **The empty chair** | A follower who doesn't engage is an empty chair. Devalues the exact number the audience obsesses over. | ⚠ **unsolved** — follower count against engagement figures | **15s** |
| **11** | **Reversed causation** | The off-topic content is not what built those audiences. **The audience is what makes the off-topic content work.** Copying it is copying the output of a system you don't have. | Dylan Francis, Diplo, John Summit, Subtronics — the pages themselves | **90s** |
| **12** | **Release *on* a label, don't get signed *to* one** | Labels care about genre release-to-release, not career-to-career. You are placing a track, not surrendering a project. | A compilation tracklist | **30s** |

**#11 is the strongest piece of analysis in the set.** Everyone says *don't copy, be original* — a platitude. This is a mechanical causal claim that explains a failure people experience constantly, and it independently reproduces the CUTDWN failure-mode rail already in [[working/WOBAR_SOCIAL_PLAN]] §3.5.

**#12 independently matches [[working/RESEARCH_LABEL_LANDSCAPE]] §2.6** — the compilation economy has ~29 slots where a signing has one. Arrived at from a different direction, which means it can be said with confidence.

## §7 — LIST 5: INTERACTIVE

*The spine, not the fifth item.*

**Why this list outranks the other four:** it is the only one where **Nick is not the authority** — nobody can object to *"go listen to two albums"* on the grounds that he has 22 monthly listeners, so the credentials objection ([[working/RESEARCH_BRAND_PSYCHOLOGY]] §2) simply does not apply. It is also the only list that is literally *interactive*, which is Joe Rath's actual definition of bucket 3 — the other four are broadcast. And it **self-feeds**: every prompt generates replies, and the replies are the next piece, at zero research cost.

| # | Piece | Claim | Object on screen | Native |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **13** | **The two-song test** | *Go listen to two songs by an artist with 10k monthly listeners. I bet you can't tell it's the same person. Tell me if I'm wrong.* | The two songs playing | **30s** |
| **14** | **The 60k rabbit hole** | Everyone studies the top. The top is unusable — different budget, team, decade. **One rung up is where transferable lessons live.** | A real page at that tier | **45s** |
| **15** | **The autopsy** | Find something that isn't working and diagnose why. Criticism training — it recruits people into the lens rather than showing them its output. | The chosen subject's material | **61s** |

**The rule that makes these work: attach a prediction you might lose.** *"Go do this and see what happens"* is too open. A falsifiable prediction is a stance and an experiment at once, and it converts disagreement into participation instead of threat.

**#14 matches the social plan's own targeting** — one rung up, 2K–20K.

## §8 — CUT, SO THEY ARE NOT RE-PROPOSED

- **AI replacing creativity.** Off-thesis, the most saturated take available, dates within a year, and starts a fight in someone else's lane. Costs more than it earns.
- **"Make it for yourself" as a purity argument.** The single most repeated sentiment in music; reads as sanctimony. **Only the mechanical version survives:** chasing an external target makes you inconsistent *because the target moves.* That version is novel and links list 2 to list 1.
- **"Most artists don't make it / good enough doesn't pop off"** as a standalone. True and completely well-trodden — unless it carries the 2026 tiny-brands evidence, which turns a platitude into a sourced argument. Only make it with the citation.

## §9 — OPEN

1. **No scripts exist. Fifteen ideas, zero written — and Nick writes them by hand** (his call, 2026-08-04). This file holds the claim, the object and the duration; the words are his. Two calibration drafts (#1, #7) were produced in session and are **not** canonical. Loop is live in [[working/WOBAR_ACTIVE]].
2. **The tie-to-music rule, decided while drafting:** the claim may be universal, **the evidence may not.** The object on screen and the examples come from music, always — then the through-line is automatic and no piece ever has to say *"and this applies to your music too,"* which is a bolt-on and reads as one. Twelve of the fifteen already pass because their object is inherently musical. **The three that fail — #1, #3, #10 — are exactly the three with no object solved.** One problem, not two. Vary *which part* of music does the work (production, DJing, releases, artwork, the page itself) or the tie-in becomes a tic.
3. **"Consistency lives below genre" has no single owner** — it is currently the substance of #4 (Post Malone) and the tail of list 4's genre argument. One of them has to give it up or the same video gets made twice.
4. **Two pieces have no object** — #3 and #10, both among the best lines. Unsolved.
5. **Duration spread is uneven by design:** belief 2 is natively short-form (images prove claims instantly), belief 1 is not (its evidence is time-based). Do not force an even distribution across the five durations — that is the container dictating the argument.
6. **Order is undecided.** #4 is the argued opener; nothing else is sequenced.
